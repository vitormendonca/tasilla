==============================================================
TASILLA - A1 CONTENT / TOPIC 20: ANIMALS & NATURE
==============================================================
Unit 4 - Wider World | Difficulty dial: Topics 16-20
(10 new words, 4 options, inference questions, natural-pace
audio ~40s, reading texts 60-80 words, dictation 6-7 words,
60-second monologues, full recycling)
THE LAST TOPIC - designed as a consolidation showcase: no
new grammar; every structure from G1-G12 appears in use.

Grammar embedded (all recycled): have/has (pets), can/
can't (animal abilities: birds can fly), there is/are
(nature), simple present vs continuous (he sleeps all
day / he is sleeping now), do/does questions
Can-do: Talk about pets, common animals and simple
nature words
Recycles: T4 (descriptions + T18 colours), T6 (under the
table!), T7 (every day), T16 (do/does, hobbies), T17
(continuous, seasons), T3 (possessive 's: my dog's name)

TARGET LANGUAGE
Words (10): animal, dog, cat, bird, fish, horse, tree,
            flower, garden, fly
Phrases:    I have a dog. / My dog's name is Bobi. /
            Birds can fly. / She is sleeping now. /
            Do you have a pet? (pet = intro-card chunk:
            an animal in your house)
Structures: nothing new - deliberate. The lesson shows
            the student how much machine they now own:
            has, is, can, does, there are, is sleeping -
            all in one topic.
NOTE: cow, lake, zoo = passive exposure. Plural fish =
fish (flagged, like feet in T19).

==============================================================
LESSON 20.1 - VOCABULARY (auto-graded, 8 exercises, all tap)
==============================================================

Intro card:
  animal = dog, cat, bird...  |  dog = your best friend!
  cat = it sleeps all day  |  bird = it can fly and sing
  fish = it lives in water (two fish - no -s!)
  horse = big, you can ride it  |  tree = big and green
  flower = small, beautiful, in the garden
  garden = the green place at a house
  fly = move in the sky (birds can!)
  + phrase bank: Do you have a pet? | My dog's name
    is... | Birds can fly!

EX 1 - Word meaning (MC)
Q: It lives in water and it can swim - but it can't
sing! It is a ___.
   a) fish   [CORRECT]
   b) bird
   c) cat
   d) horse
Feedback: Fish -> water. Bird -> sky and songs!

EX 2 - Word meaning (MC)
Q: Big and green, with birds in it: a ___.
   a) tree   [CORRECT]
   b) flower
   c) garden
   d) dog
Feedback: Tree = big. Flower = small. Garden = the
whole green place!

EX 3 - Gap-fill (tap the missing word)
Q: Birds can ___ - up, up in the sky!
   a) fly   [CORRECT]
   b) swim
   c) read
   d) buy
Feedback: Fly = move in the sky. Fish swim, birds fly!

EX 4 - Gap-fill (tap the missing word)
Q: There are red and white flowers in my ___.
   a) garden   [CORRECT]
   b) bedroom
   c) office
   d) pharmacy
Feedback: Garden = the green place at a house (T6
neighbor!).

EX 5 - Odd one out (MC)
Q: Which one is NOT an animal?
   a) horse
   b) flower   [CORRECT]
   c) bird
   d) cat
Feedback: A flower is nature - but not an animal!

EX 6 - Dialogue completion (MC)
Q: A: Do you have a pet?  B: ___
   a) Yes, I do! I have a small dog.   [CORRECT]
   b) Yes, I am.
   c) No, it is closed.
   d) It is raining.
Feedback: Do you have...? -> Yes, I do / No, I don't
(T16 - forever!).

EX 7 - GRAMMAR: can + ability (MC)
Q: Birds ___ fly, but they ___ swim like fish!
   a) can / can't   [CORRECT]
   b) can't / can
   c) can / don't
   d) do / can't
Feedback: can = yes ability, can't = no ability (T14 +
T19).

EX 8 - GRAMMAR: the full machine (MC)
Q: My cat ___ ten years old. She ___ green eyes, and
right now she ___ on my bed!
   a) is / has / is sleeping   [CORRECT]
   b) has / is / sleeps
   c) is / have / sleeping
   d) is / has / sleeps
Feedback: IS + age (T2), HAS + eyes (T4), IS SLEEPING +
now (T17). Three topics, one cat - look how much you
know!

Pass: 70% (6 of 8). XP: 10.

LESSON 20.2 - LISTENING (auto-graded, 1 audio + 5 exercises)
==============================================================

AUDIO SCRIPT (to record - 1 speaker, natural pace -
a monologue, like Topic 1: full circle!,
file: audio/a1/t20_listening_my_animals.mp3, ~40 sec):

  Hello! Do you like animals? I love them! I have a dog
  and two cats. My dog's name is Bobi - he is big, and
  he is very funny. He can swim! We go to the park every
  morning. And right now? Ha - he is sleeping under the
  table. My cats don't like water, but they can go up
  the trees - very fast! And you? Do you have an animal
  at home?

Max plays: 3.

EX 1 - Gist (MC)
Q: What is this audio?
   a) A person talks about her animals   [CORRECT]
   b) A visit to the pharmacy
   c) A weather report
   d) Directions to a park

EX 2 - Detail (MC)
Q: What animals does the speaker have?
   a) One dog and two cats   [CORRECT]
   b) Two dogs and one cat
   c) A dog and a bird
   d) Two fish

EX 3 - Detail: can (MC)
Q: What can Bobi do?
   a) Swim   [CORRECT]
   b) Fly
   c) Sing
   d) Go up trees

EX 4 - Detail: continuous + T6 (MC)
Q: What is Bobi doing right now?
   a) Sleeping under the table   [CORRECT]
   b) Swimming in the park
   c) Playing with the cats
   d) Eating

EX 5 - DICTATION (type what you hear - 7 words)
Audio repeats: He is sleeping under the table.
Correct answer: He is sleeping under the table
(accept case-insensitive, ignore punctuation)

Pass: 70% (4 of 5). XP: 10.

LESSON 20.3 - READING (auto-graded, 2 texts, 3 questions each)
==============================================================

TEXT A (64 words) - Park notice format:
---------------------------------------
  WELCOME TO CITY PARK!
  There are big trees and gardens with red and white
  flowers.
  Our birds sing every morning - come and listen!
  In the water: fish! Please DON'T give them bread.
  You can see the horses every day at ten o'clock.
  Open every day, seven to seven.
  Dogs are OK - with you, on the street side!
---------------------------------------

EX 1 - Gist (MC)
Q: What is this text?
   a) A notice at a city park   [CORRECT]
   b) A zoo ticket
   c) A pet shop flyer
   d) A weather report

EX 2 - Detail (MC)
Q: What time can you see the horses?
   a) Ten o'clock, every day   [CORRECT]
   b) Seven o'clock
   c) Only on the weekend
   d) The text does not say

EX 3 - Detail: imperative (MC)
Q: What is the rule about the fish?
   a) Don't give them bread   [CORRECT]
   b) Don't swim with them
   c) Give them bread every morning
   d) Fish are not in this park

TEXT B (70 words) - Chat messages format:
---------------------------------------
  Tuca: Nina!! You have a new cat?!
  Nina: Yes! Her name is Luna. She is white, with four
        black feet - little black shoes!
  Tuca: Ha! Is she big?
  Nina: No, small. Look at the photo - she is playing
        with my shoe right now!
  Tuca: And your dog? Does he like Luna?
  Nina: No, he doesn't! He is old. He doesn't play -
        he sleeps all day.
---------------------------------------

EX 4 - Gist (MC)
Q: What is Nina's news?
   a) She has a new cat, Luna   [CORRECT]
   b) She has a new dog
   c) Her dog is sick
   d) She is at the park

EX 5 - Detail: description + colours (MC)
Q: What does Luna look like?
   a) White, with four black feet   [CORRECT]
   b) Black, with white feet
   c) Big and old
   d) The text does not say

EX 6 - INFERENCE (MC)
Q: Why does the old dog NOT play with Luna?
   a) He is old and sleeps all day - playing is not
      his life now   [CORRECT]
   b) He doesn't live with Nina
   c) Luna is too big
   d) He plays with shoes
Feedback: Old + sleeps all day + doesn't play - add it
up. Poor Luna, she plays alone!

Pass: 70% (5 of 6). XP: 10.

LESSON 20.5 - WRITING (teacher-graded, MODE B - HANDWRITTEN,
FREE-WRITE - THE LAST PAPER TASK)
==============================================================

EIGHTH AND FINAL PAPER TASK. Student writes BY HAND,
then uploads a photo / scan / PDF.

TASK: Describe a pet or an animal, handwritten.

Instructions shown in app:
  1. Choose a real animal: your pet, a family pet, or
     an animal from your street or a park.
  2. On paper, write 6-8 sentences about it BY HAND.
  3. Draw it too if you like (optional - the sentences
     are the task!).
  4. Take a photo and upload it.

Minimum requirements:
  - 6-8 sentences, by hand
  - name with possessive 's (My cat's name is...) OR
    has + description
  - one colour + one size word (T18 + T4)
  - one can or can't sentence
  - one habit (does every day) AND one is doing now
    sentence if possible

RUBRIC:
  1. 6-8 sentences, real animal             [ pass / not yet ]
  2. is/has description + colour correct    [ pass / not yet ]
  3. can/can't + present forms correct      [ pass / not yet ]
  4. Handwriting readable (A1 bar)          [ pass / not yet ]

Teacher view: opens image/PDF + rubric side by side.
Approve / Request redo (+ feedback).
XP: 15 on submit, +10 on approval.

DEPENDENCY NOTE: requires writing-upload feature. Until
built, runs temporarily as typed Mode A with the same
requirements.
