TASILLA - A1 FINAL TESTS / READING PATH FINAL TEST
(STEP 27 OF 27 OF THE READING SKILL PATH)
==============================================================
Covers: Topics 1-20. Passing this test completes the
Reading path and feeds the Reading skill score (gated
at 70%).

DESIGN:
- 3 new texts in three real-life formats, difficulty
  ramping like the course: profile (Units 1-2 language),
  notice (Unit 3), personal letter (Unit 4 + full
  recycling)
- 20 items: 6 + 6 + 8
- Pass 70% (14 of 20). Attempts: 2, then teacher unlock.
  XP: 50. Time guide: ~20 minutes, untimed.
