==============================================================
TASILLA - A1 CONTENT / TOPIC 12: PLACES IN TOWN & DIRECTIONS
==============================================================
Unit 3 - Out in the World | Difficulty dial: Topics 11-15
(8 new words, 4 options, 'not stated' live, audio ~35s
dialogues, reading texts 50-60 words, dictation 6 words,
free-write)

Grammar embedded: IMPERATIVES (G12: Turn left. Go straight.)
+ prepositions of place extended (G6: next to, near) +
there is/are and Where is...? recycled (G5)
Can-do: Identify places in town, ask for and follow
simple directions
Recycles: T6 (there is/are, in/on/under), T11 (shop,
market, open/closed), T8 (days/times), T2 (city, street
address idea)

TARGET LANGUAGE
Words (8): street, bank, hospital, supermarket, park,
           station, left, right
Phrases:   Excuse me, where is the bank? / Go straight. /
           Turn left. / Turn right. / It is next to the
           park. / It is near. / Thank you!
Structures: imperative = verb first, no subject
            (Turn left! Go straight!)
            next to + place / near (no object needed)
NOTE: school is promoted from sight word (used since T1)
to active vocabulary here as a PLACE. in front of / behind
/ between NOT taught (kept for A1 review margin - next to
and near carry A1 directions). bus/train specifics are
Topic 13 - station here is just a place in town.

==============================================================
LESSON 12.1 - VOCABULARY (auto-graded, 8 exercises, all tap)
==============================================================

Intro card:
  street = cars and houses are on it
  bank = your money is there  |  hospital = doctors work
  there  |  supermarket = a big food shop
  park = green place, trees  |  station = trains and
  buses go from there
  left = <- this side  |  right = this side ->
  + phrase bank: Excuse me, where is...? | Go straight. |
    Turn left/right. | next to | near

EX 1 - Word meaning (MC)
Q: Your money is in the ___.
   a) bank   [CORRECT]
   b) park
   c) hospital
   d) street
Feedback: Bank = the money place (T9 money!).

EX 2 - Word meaning (MC)
Q: A big shop with all the food is a ___.
   a) supermarket   [CORRECT]
   b) station
   c) bank
   d) bag
Feedback: Supermarket = big food shop. Market (T11) = many
small shops.

EX 3 - Gap-fill (tap the missing word)
Q: Trains go from the ___.
   a) station   [CORRECT]
   b) park
   c) bedroom
   d) menu
Feedback: Station = trains and buses start there.

EX 4 - Gap-fill (tap the missing word)
Q: The trees are green and the children play. It is a ___.
   a) park   [CORRECT]
   b) hospital
   c) supermarket
   d) size
Feedback: Park = the green place in town.

EX 5 - Odd one out (MC)
Q: Which word is NOT a place in town?
   a) hospital
   b) thirsty   [CORRECT]
   c) bank
   d) park
Feedback: Thirsty is a feeling (Topic 5) - not a place.

EX 6 - Dialogue completion (MC)
Q: A: Excuse me, where is the bank?  B: ___
   a) Go straight and turn left. It is next to the park.
      [CORRECT]
   b) It is thirty dollars.
   c) I am fine, thank you.
   d) There are two bedrooms.
Feedback: Where is...? -> answer with directions!

EX 7 - GRAMMAR: imperatives (MC)
Q: Which one is a direction (an instruction)?
   a) Turn right at the supermarket!   [CORRECT]
   b) I turn right every day.
   c) She turns right.
   d) The supermarket is right.
Feedback: Instructions start with the VERB: Turn...! Go...!

EX 8 - GRAMMAR: next to / near / under (MC)
Q: The bank is ___ the park - they are on the same street.
   a) next to   [CORRECT]
   b) under
   c) in
   d) at
Feedback: Next to = at the side of. Under (T6) = below!

Pass: 70% (6 of 8). XP: 10.

LESSON 12.4 - SPEAKING (teacher-graded, 1 recorded task)
==============================================================

PROMPT (no example - free stage):
  Record yourself. A friend is at the station in YOUR
  town. Give directions to your home (or your school).
  Include:
  1. A start: OK! From the station...
  2. Two or more instructions: Go straight... Turn left/
     right at the...
  3. One place from this topic on the way (supermarket,
     park, bank...)
  4. The end: My house is next to / near...

Max recording: 60 seconds.
Tip: Move your hand left and right while you speak -
it helps!

RUBRIC:
  1. 2+ imperatives (Turn..., Go...)        [ pass / not yet ]
  2. next to / near used correctly          [ pass / not yet ]
  3. Real places, logical order             [ pass / not yet ]
  4. Understandable pronunciation           [ pass / not yet ]

XP: 15 on submit, +10 on approval.

==============================================================
LESSON 12.5 - WRITING (teacher-graded, MODE B - HANDWRITTEN,
FREE-WRITE)
==============================================================

FIFTH PAPER TASK. Student writes BY HAND on paper, then
uploads a photo / scan / PDF.

TASK: Hand-write directions from A to B.

Instructions shown in app:
  1. Choose a real route you know: your home -> school,
     home -> supermarket, station -> home...
  2. On paper, write the title: HOW TO GO FROM ... TO ...
  3. Write the directions as a numbered list (3-5 steps),
     like a note for a friend.
  4. Take a photo and upload it.

Minimum requirements:
  - Title with the two places
  - 3-5 numbered steps with imperatives (Go..., Turn...)
  - next to or near at least once
  - One place word from this topic

RUBRIC:
  1. Title + numbered steps format          [ pass / not yet ]
  2. Imperatives correct (verb first)       [ pass / not yet ]
  3. next to / near + place words correct   [ pass / not yet ]
  4. Handwriting readable (A1 bar)          [ pass / not yet ]

Teacher view: opens image/PDF + rubric side by side.
Approve / Request redo (+ feedback).
XP: 15 on submit, +10 on approval.

DEPENDENCY NOTE: requires writing-upload feature. Until
built, runs temporarily as typed Mode A with the same
numbered-list format.

PRODUCTION NOTES
==============================================================
- 1 audio to record (~35s, 2 speakers) + 1 review line (~12s)
- Excuse me = taught inside phrase bank (courtesy chunk,
  like Nice to meet you in T1)
- minutes, tourist, tree = passive exposure, never tested
- in front of / behind / between deliberately NOT taught -
  A1 certificate needs next to + near only; do not let
  them creep into options
- Imperatives are positive only (Turn, Go); negative
  imperative (Don't...) waits for do-support awareness
  (post-T16, used in T15 classroom passively)
- Recycling check: there is/are everywhere; T11 shop/market
  + open/closed; T9 numbers (house number 15/50 danger
  pair); T8 six o'clock
