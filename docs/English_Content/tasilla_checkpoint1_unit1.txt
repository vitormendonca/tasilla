SECTION 1 - LISTENING (7 items, 1 new audio)
==============================================================

AUDIO SCRIPT (to record - 1 speaker, slow, clear pauses,
file: audio/a1/checkpoint1_listening.mp3, ~35 sec):

  Good morning! | My name is Alba. A - L - B - A. | I am 28
  years old and I am from Santos. | This is my brother, Nuno.
  | He is tall and he has long hair. | Today he is tired and
  hungry - but he is not sad!

Max plays: 2 (checkpoint allowance is stricter than lessons).

EX 1 (T1) - Gist: What is this audio?
   a) A person introduces herself and her brother  [CORRECT]
   b) A person says goodbye
   c) A person orders food

   a) True   [CORRECT]  (Good morning!)
   b) False

EX 3 (T2) - How does she spell her name?
   a) A - L - B - A   [CORRECT]
   b) A - L - V - A
   c) A - B - L - A

EX 4 (T2) - Where is Alba from?
   a) Santos   [CORRECT]
   b) Salvador
   c) The audio does not say

EX 5 (T3) - Who is Nuno?
   a) Alba's brother   [CORRECT]
   b) Alba's father
   c) Alba's friend

EX 6 (T4/T5) - What does Nuno look like, and how is he today?
   a) Tall with long hair; tired and hungry   [CORRECT]
   b) Short with short hair; happy
   c) Tall with long hair; sad

EX 7 - DICTATION (type what you hear - 5 words)
Audio repeats: I am from Santos.
Correct answer: I am from Santos
(accept case-insensitive, ignore final punctuation)
