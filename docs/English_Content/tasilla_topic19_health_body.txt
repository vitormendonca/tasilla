==============================================================
TASILLA - A1 CONTENT / TOPIC 19: HEALTH & BODY
==============================================================
Unit 4 - Wider World | Difficulty dial: Topics 16-20
(10 new words, 4 options, inference questions, natural-pace
audio ~40s, reading texts 60-80 words, dictation 6-7 words,
60-second monologues, full recycling)

Grammar embedded: HAVE/HAS for symptoms (I have a headache -
extends T4's have for features) + CAN'T (the negative of
G11: I can't go to work) + imperatives for advice (G12:
Drink water! Go to bed! + first light use of Don't...,
now that do-support is known from T16)
Can-do: Say what hurts, name body parts, manage a
pharmacy visit
Recycles: T4 (have/has), T5 (I am + state: sick joins
tired/cold), T12 (pharmacy joins places, next to), T14
(can), T16 (do/does -> don't as advice), T17 (continuous)

TARGET LANGUAGE
Words (10): body, head, arm, leg, hand, foot, sick,
            headache, pharmacy, medicine
Phrases:    I am sick. / I have a headache. / I can't go
            to work today. / Take one with water. /
            Go to bed and sleep! / Is there a pharmacy
            near here?
Structures: have/has + a + symptom (I have a headache /
            she has a headache)
            can't + verb (I can't come to class)
            advice imperatives: Drink...! Sleep!
            Don't watch TV all day!
NOTE: foot -> FEET flagged on the intro card (like shoes
in T18: my feet ARE cold). hurts (my leg hurts) NOT
taught - A1 bar covered by I have a... + my leg is bad/
tired. should (advice) is A2 - advice is imperatives
only. Doctor/hospital recycled from T14/T12.

==============================================================
LESSON 19.1 - VOCABULARY (auto-graded, 8 exercises, all tap)
==============================================================

Intro card:
  body = ALL of you!  |  head = eyes, hair - the top!
  arm = shoulder to hand  |  leg = it walks!
  hand = five fingers  |  foot = it is in your shoe
  (two = FEET!)
  sick = not OK, not well  |  headache = your head is
  bad - ouch!
  pharmacy = the medicine shop  |  medicine = you take
  it when you are sick
  + phrase bank: I have a headache. | I can't go to
    work. | Go to bed!

EX 1 - Word meaning (MC)
Q: Your ___ is in your shoe. Your ___ has five fingers.
   a) foot / hand   [CORRECT]
   b) hand / foot
   c) head / leg
   d) arm / foot
Feedback: Foot -> shoe (T18!). Hand -> fingers.

EX 2 - Word meaning (MC)
Q: You are not OK today - hot head, tired body. You
are ___.
   a) sick   [CORRECT]
   b) happy
   c) closed
   d) expensive
Feedback: Sick = not well. (I am sick - like I am tired,
T5!)

EX 3 - Gap-fill (tap the missing word)
Q: The medicine shop is the ___.
   a) pharmacy   [CORRECT]
   b) supermarket
   c) station
   d) office
Feedback: Pharmacy = medicine. Supermarket (T12) = food!

EX 4 - Gap-fill (tap the missing word)
Q: I have a headache. I take my ___ with water.
   a) medicine   [CORRECT]
   b) breakfast
   c) juice
   d) paper
Feedback: Medicine = you take it when you are sick.

EX 5 - Odd one out (MC)
Q: Which one is NOT a part of the body?
   a) arm
   b) jacket   [CORRECT]
   c) leg
   d) head
Feedback: A jacket (T18) goes ON the body - it is
clothes!

EX 6 - Dialogue completion (MC)
Q: A: I am sick... I have a headache.  B: ___
   a) Go to bed and sleep! And drink water.   [CORRECT]
   b) Turn left at the bank.
   c) It is forty dollars.
   d) Yes, I do.
Feedback: A sick friend -> give advice with imperatives
(T12 grammar, new job!).

EX 7 - GRAMMAR: have/has + symptom (MC)
Q: I ___ a headache. My brother ___ a headache too -
what a day!
   a) have / has   [CORRECT]
   b) has / have
   c) am / is
   d) have / have
Feedback: I HAVE, he HAS - same rule as T4 (long hair),
new use (headache!).

EX 8 - GRAMMAR: can't (MC)
Q: I am sick, so I ___ go to work today. But I ___
watch films at home!
   a) can't / can   [CORRECT]
   b) can / can't
   c) don't / do
   d) can't / can't
Feedback: can't = can NOT. Sick -> can't work... but CAN
rest!

Pass: 70% (6 of 8). XP: 10.

LESSON 19.2 - LISTENING (auto-graded, 1 audio + 5 exercises)
==============================================================

AUDIO SCRIPT (to record - 2 speakers, natural pace, phone
call, file: audio/a1/t19_listening_i_am_sick.mp3, ~40 sec):

  Zilda: Hello, Beto! Where are you? Everyone is at
         the office!
  Beto:  I am at home, Zilda. I am sick today. I have a
         headache, and my body is tired, tired, tired...
  Zilda: Oh no! So you can't work today?
  Beto:  No, I can't. I can't go to work today. Sorry!
  Zilda: Don't say sorry! Go to bed, drink water, and
         sleep.
  Beto:  Thank you. My sister is at the pharmacy now -
         she is buying my medicine.

Max plays: 3.

EX 1 - Gist (MC)
Q: Why does Zilda call Beto?
   a) Because he is not at the office   [CORRECT]
   b) Because she is sick
   c) To buy medicine
   d) To talk about the weekend

EX 2 - Detail: symptom (MC)
Q: What is wrong with Beto?
   a) He has a headache and a tired body   [CORRECT]
   b) His leg is bad
   c) He is cold
   d) The audio does not say

EX 3 - Detail: can't (MC)
Q: Can Beto work today?
   a) No, he can't   [CORRECT]
   b) Yes, he can
   c) Only in the morning
   d) The audio does not say

EX 4 - INFERENCE (MC)
Q: Why is Beto's sister at the pharmacy?
   a) Because Beto is sick and needs medicine   [CORRECT]
   b) Because she is sick
   c) Because she works there
   d) Because it is next to her house
Feedback: Beto sick + sister buying medicine -> she is
helping her brother. One step!

EX 5 - DICTATION (type what you hear - 7 words)
Audio repeats: I can't go to work today.
Correct answer: I can't go to work today
(accept case-insensitive, ignore punctuation; accept
cant for can't)

Pass: 70% (4 of 5). XP: 10.

LESSON 19.3 - READING (auto-graded, 2 texts, 3 questions each)
==============================================================

TEXT A (62 words) - Medicine label format:
---------------------------------------
  MEDICINE - FOR HEADACHE
  Take ONE with water, in the morning.
  Take ONE at night.
  DON'T take three in one day!
  Not for children under twelve.
  Sick for three days? Go to the doctor!
  Questions? Ask us at PHARMACY BELA - next to the
  supermarket, open every day, eight to eight.
---------------------------------------

EX 1 - Gist (MC)
Q: What is this text?
   a) Instructions on a medicine box   [CORRECT]
   b) A shopping list
   c) A doctor's profile
   d) A school notice

EX 2 - Detail (MC)
Q: How does a person take this medicine?
   a) One in the morning, one at night, with water
      [CORRECT]
   b) Three in one day
   c) Only at night
   d) The text does not say

EX 3 - INFERENCE (MC)
Q: You take the medicine, but you are sick for three
days. What is the right thing to do?
   a) Go to the doctor   [CORRECT]
   b) Take three in one day
   c) Give it to a child
   d) Go to the supermarket
Feedback: The label says it: sick for three days ->
doctor!

TEXT B (66 words) - Chat messages format:
---------------------------------------
  Duna: Vito?! You are not at school today. Are you OK?
  Vito: I am sick, Duna. I have a headache, and my legs
        are very tired.
  Duna: Oh no! Do you have medicine at home?
  Vito: Yes. My mother is at the pharmacy now - more
        medicine for tonight.
  Duna: Good. Now sleep! And DON'T watch films all day!
  Vito: Ha!... OK. One film. ONE!
---------------------------------------

EX 4 - Gist (MC)
Q: Why does Duna write to Vito?
   a) He is not at school, and she asks how he is
      [CORRECT]
   b) She needs the homework
   c) She is at the pharmacy
   d) She wants to watch a film

EX 5 - Detail (MC)
Q: Who is at the pharmacy?
   a) Vito's mother   [CORRECT]
   b) Vito
   c) Duna
   d) Vito's sister

EX 6 - INFERENCE (MC)
Q: Duna says: DON'T watch films all day! What does this
tell us about Vito?
   a) Films are his big hobby - he watches a lot
      [CORRECT]
   b) He doesn't like films
   c) He has no TV at home
   d) He is at the cinema
Feedback: She knows him - and his answer ('OK. One
film!') tells the story. Classic T16 hobby, spotted by
inference!

Pass: 70% (5 of 6). XP: 10.

LESSON 19.4 - SPEAKING (teacher-graded, 1 recorded task)
==============================================================

PROMPT (no example - free stage; 60-second monologue):
  Record a 60-second voice message: YOU ARE SICK TODAY.
  Imagine you send this to your teacher or your boss.
  Cover:
  1. Greeting + the news: I am sick today.
  2. What is wrong: I have a... / my ... is ...
     (2 details)
  3. What you can't do today: I can't come to... /
     I can't...
  4. What is happening now: My mother is buying
     medicine... / I am drinking tea... (T17 -ing!)
  5. A polite end: Sorry! See you on... (day - T8)

Max recording: 75 seconds (target: fill 60).
Tip: This is a REAL message people send - record it
like it is true.
