TASILLA - A1 REVIEWS / MIXED REVIEW B (ROADMAP ONLY)
COVERS TOPICS 4-5
==============================================================
Topics covered: 4 Describing People / 5 Feelings & States
Grammar covered: to be full paradigm (all persons, questions,
negatives, short answers), have/has for features
Position in roadmap: after Topic 5, before Checkpoint 1

DESIGN (template = Mixed Review A):
- 12 items: 3 listening + 3 reading + 4 vocabulary/grammar +
  1 speaking + 1 writing (teacher-graded, async)
- All content NEW - never copies of lesson items
- Auto-graded part gates progression (pass 70% = 7 of 10);
  speaking/writing async, never block the roadmap
- XP 20

==============================================================
SECTION A - LISTENING (3 items, 1 new audio ~20s)
==============================================================

AUDIO SCRIPT (to record - 1 speaker, slow,
file: audio/a1/mixrevB_listening.mp3):

  Hello! My name is Iva. | This is my friend Gustavo. |
  He is tall and he has short hair. | He is tired today,
  but he is very happy!

EX 1 - Who is Gustavo?
   a) Iva's friend   [CORRECT]
   b) Iva's brother
   c) Iva's father

EX 2 - What does Gustavo look like?
   a) Tall, with short hair   [CORRECT]
   b) Short, with long hair
   c) The audio does not describe him

EX 3 - T/F: Gustavo is sad today.
   a) True
   b) False   [CORRECT]  (tired, but very HAPPY)

==============================================================
SECTION B - READING (3 items, 1 new mini-text)
==============================================================

Text (33 words) - Social post format:
---------------------------------------
  Hi! I am Nilo. I am short and I have big eyes.
  Today I am very happy - my sister is here!
  Her name is Vane. She is tall and very nice.
---------------------------------------

EX 4 - Gist: What is this text?
   a) Nilo describes himself and his sister   [CORRECT]
   b) A goodbye message
   c) A registration form

EX 5 - Who is Vane?
   a) Nilo's sister   [CORRECT]
   b) Nilo's friend
   c) Nilo's mother

EX 6 - What does Vane look like?
   a) She is tall   [CORRECT]
   b) She is short
   c) She has big eyes

==============================================================
SECTION C - VOCABULARY & GRAMMAR (4 items)
==============================================================

EX 7 - Q: I ___ short hair. My mother ___ long hair.
   a) have / has   [CORRECT]
   b) has / have
   c) have / have

EX 8 - Q: A: ___ you hungry?  B: Yes, I ___.
   a) Are / am   [CORRECT]
   b) Is / are
   c) Are / is

EX 9 - Q: You need water. You are ___.
   a) thirsty   [CORRECT]
   b) hungry
   c) tall

EX 10 - Q: My parents ___ very nice.
   a) are   [CORRECT]
   b) is
   c) am

==============================================================
SECTION D - SPEAKING (1 item, teacher-graded, async)
==============================================================

EX 11: Record 20-30 seconds. Choose ONE person you know.
  Say: who they are + name (T3), what they look like
  (is + adjective, has + hair/eyes - T4), and how they are
  today (feeling word - T5).
  Rubric: describes appearance / gives a feeling /
  to be + have/has correct / understandable.

==============================================================
SECTION E - WRITING (1 item, teacher-graded, async)
==============================================================

EX 12: A friend writes to you:
  Hi! How are you today? And what does your best friend
  look like?
  Reply in 3-4 sentences (2 feeling words about you + 2
  description sentences about your friend with is/has).
  Rubric: replies to both questions / is + adjective and
  has + feature correct / spelling A1 OK.

Auto-graded pass: 70% (7 of 10). XP: 20.
