SECTION A - LISTENING (3 items, 1 new audio ~30s)
==============================================================

AUDIO SCRIPT (to record - 2 speakers, natural pace,
file: audio/a1/mixrevG_listening.mp3):

  Pipa: Hi, Ciro! It is raining - no park today!
  Ciro: I know... so I am at home. I am watching an old
        film with my dog!
  Pipa: Ha! Does your dog like films?
  Ciro: Yes, he does! But he can't watch all day - he
        sleeps.
  Pipa: And what are you wearing? It is cold today!
  Ciro: My big blue jacket, Mama Pipa! Don't worry!

EX 1 - Gist: What is this conversation?
   a) A rainy day at home, with a film and a dog
      [CORRECT]
   b) A trip to the park
   c) Buying a jacket in a shop
   d) A sick day at the pharmacy

EX 2 - What is Ciro doing?
   a) Watching an old film with his dog   [CORRECT]
   b) Walking in the park
   c) Sleeping
   d) Playing games

EX 3 - What is Ciro wearing?
   a) His big blue jacket   [CORRECT]
   b) A white shirt
   c) His winter hat
   d) The audio does not say
