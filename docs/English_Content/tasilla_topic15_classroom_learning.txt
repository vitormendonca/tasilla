==============================================================
TASILLA - A1 CONTENT / TOPIC 15: CLASSROOM & LEARNING
==============================================================
Unit 3 - Out in the World | Difficulty dial: Topics 11-15
(8 new words, 4 options, 'not stated' live, audio ~35s
dialogues, reading texts 50-60 words, dictation ~6 words,
free-write)

Grammar embedded: IMPERATIVES + OBJECT PRONOUNS (G12
complete: Open it! Read them! Help me!) + classroom
survival CHUNKS (How do you spell...? / What does ...
mean? - taught as fixed phrases; their do/does grammar
is analyzed in Topic 16)
Can-do: Use classroom language - instructions, asking
about spelling and meaning
Recycles: T12 (imperatives), T14 (can, help, speak,
write), T2 (spelling aloud), T13 (ticket, station reused
as example words), T8 (days)

TARGET LANGUAGE
Words (8): book, pen, paper, question, answer, word,
           read, listen
Phrases (as chunks): Open your book. / Listen and repeat. /
           How do you spell...? / What does ... mean? /
           Sorry, can you repeat, please? / Good question!
Structures: imperative + object pronoun:
            Open it. Read them. Help me. Listen to me.
            object pronouns: me, it, them (core set;
            him/her/us noted on intro card)
NOTE ON CHUNKS: How do you spell...? and What does ...
mean? contain do/does. They are taught HERE as unanalyzed
survival phrases (memorized like Nice to meet you in T1).
Topic 16 opens the grammar box. Students are NEVER asked
to build new do-questions in Topic 15.

==============================================================
LESSON 15.1 - VOCABULARY (auto-graded, 8 exercises, all tap)
==============================================================

Intro card:
  book = you read it (open your book!)
  pen = you write with it  |  paper = you write on it
  question = Where...? What...? -> ?
  answer = your response to a question
  word = hello is a word!  |  read = eyes on the words
  listen = ears on! (listen to the audio)
  + phrase bank: How do you spell...? |
    What does ... mean? | Sorry, can you repeat, please?
  + grammar note: Open IT (the book). Read THEM (the
    words). Help ME!

EX 1 - Word meaning (MC)
Q: You write with a ___ on paper.
   a) pen   [CORRECT]
   b) book
   c) word
   d) chair
Feedback: Pen -> you write WITH it. Paper -> you write ON it.

EX 2 - Word meaning (MC)
Q: 'Where is the bank?' is a ___. 'Next to the park.' is
the ___.
   a) question / answer   [CORRECT]
   b) answer / question
   c) word / book
   d) pen / paper
Feedback: Question = ? Answer = the response.

EX 3 - Gap-fill (tap the missing word)
Q: ___ to the audio, then answer the questions.
   a) Listen   [CORRECT]
   b) Read
   c) Eat
   d) Buy
Feedback: Listen = ears! Read = eyes!

EX 4 - Gap-fill (tap the missing word)
Q: 'Goodbye' is an English ___.
   a) word   [CORRECT]
   b) paper
   c) answer
   d) office
Feedback: Hello, goodbye, ticket... all words!

EX 5 - Odd one out (MC)
Q: Which one is NOT a classroom thing?
   a) book
   b) ticket   [CORRECT]
   c) pen
   d) paper
Feedback: Ticket is for the bus (Topic 13)!

EX 6 - Dialogue completion: survival chunks (MC)
Q: Teacher: The word is 'airport'.  You: ___
   a) How do you spell it?   [CORRECT]
   b) Turn left at the park.
   c) I am from Brazil.
   d) Two tickets, please.
Feedback: New word? Ask: How do you spell it? (chunk -
learn it whole!)

EX 7 - GRAMMAR: object pronouns (MC)
Q: The book, please - open ___. The words - read ___.
   a) it / them   [CORRECT]
   b) them / it
   c) it / they
   d) him / them
Feedback: The book = IT. The words (many) = THEM.

EX 8 - GRAMMAR: imperative + pronoun (MC)
Q: I don't understand this question! Teacher, can you
help ___?
   a) me   [CORRECT]
   b) I
   c) my
   d) them
Feedback: Help ME (not help I!). I -> me after a verb.

Pass: 70% (6 of 8). XP: 10.

LESSON 15.2 - LISTENING (auto-graded, 1 audio + 5 exercises)
==============================================================

AUDIO SCRIPT (to record - 2 speakers, slow-natural,
file: audio/a1/t15_listening_in_class.mp3, ~35 sec):

  Teacher: Good morning, class! Open your books, please.
           Page thirteen.
  Nel:     Sorry, can you repeat, please?
  Teacher: Of course! Page thirteen - one, three. Now
           listen and read the words with me.
  Nel:     Teacher, one question. What does 'ticket' mean?
  Teacher: Good question, Nel! A ticket - you buy it at
           the station, for the bus or the train.

Max plays: 3.

EX 1 - Gist (MC)
Q: Where is this conversation?
   a) In an English class   [CORRECT]
   b) At a ticket office
   c) In a shop
   d) At a hospital

EX 2 - Detail: number danger pair (MC)
Q: What page is the class on?
   a) Thirteen   [CORRECT]
   b) Thirty
   c) Three
   d) The audio does not say

EX 3 - Detail (MC)
Q: What does Nel ask about the word 'ticket'?
   a) What it means   [CORRECT]
   b) How to spell it
   c) How much it is
   d) Where the station is

EX 4 - NOT STATED (MC)
Q: How many students are in the class?
   a) Thirteen
   b) Three
   c) Thirty
   d) The audio does not say   [CORRECT]

EX 5 - DICTATION (type what you hear - 5 words)
Audio repeats: Sorry, can you repeat, please?
Correct answer: Sorry can you repeat please
(accept case-insensitive, ignore punctuation)

Pass: 70% (4 of 5). XP: 10.

LESSON 15.3 - READING (auto-graded, 2 texts, 3 questions each)
==============================================================

TEXT A (50 words) - Class notes format:
---------------------------------------
  ENGLISH CLASS - MY NOTES (Monday)
  New words: station, ticket, travel
  Phrase of the day: 'A ticket to Olinda, please!'
  For Thursday: read pages twenty and twenty-one.
  Answer questions one to five on paper.
  Remember: How do you spell it? = S-P-E-L-L!
---------------------------------------

EX 1 - Gist (MC)
Q: What is this text?
   a) A student's notes from English class   [CORRECT]
   b) A bus timetable
   c) A job advertisement
   d) A cafe menu

EX 2 - Detail (MC)
Q: What are the new words in these notes?
   a) station, ticket, travel   [CORRECT]
   b) book, pen, paper
   c) Olinda, Thursday, Monday
   d) The text does not say

EX 3 - Detail (MC)
Q: What pages does the student read for Thursday?
   a) Twenty and twenty-one   [CORRECT]
   b) Twelve and thirteen
   c) One to five
   d) The text does not say

TEXT B (54 words) - Chat messages format:
---------------------------------------
  Pam:  Zeca! The English homework - is it question
        three?
  Zeca: No! Questions four and five. Read them at night,
        it is easy.
  Pam:  OK. One thing: what does 'help' mean?
  Zeca: Help = you do good things for a person. Ask me -
        I can help you!
  Pam:  Ha! You are a good teacher, Zeca!
---------------------------------------

EX 4 - Gist (MC)
Q: What are Pam and Zeca talking about?
   a) The English homework   [CORRECT]
   b) A trip by train
   c) Zeca's job
   d) A new book shop

EX 5 - Detail (MC)
Q: What is the homework?
   a) Questions four and five   [CORRECT]
   b) Question three
   c) Pages four and five
   d) The text does not say

EX 6 - Grammar in context: object pronoun (MC)
Q: Zeca says: Read ___ at night. (= questions four
and five)
   a) them   [CORRECT]
   b) it
   c) they
   d) him
Feedback: Two questions = THEM.

Pass: 70% (5 of 6). XP: 10.

LESSON 15.4 - SPEAKING (teacher-graded, 1 recorded task)
==============================================================

PROMPT (no example - free stage):
  Record yourself. Classroom theater - two voices again!
  TEACHER voice: give 2 instructions with pronouns
  (Open your book... read it! Listen to me!)
  STUDENT voice: ask the 3 survival questions:
  1. Sorry, can you repeat, please?
  2. How do you spell ___? (choose a real word)
  3. What does ___ mean? (choose another real word)

Max recording: 60 seconds.
Tip: These 3 questions save you in EVERY English class -
learn them like a song!

LESSON 15.5 - WRITING (teacher-graded, MODE B - HANDWRITTEN,
FREE-WRITE)
==============================================================

SIXTH PAPER TASK. Student writes BY HAND on paper, then
uploads a photo / scan / PDF.

TASK: Copy and complete a set of class notes.

Instructions shown in app:
  1. Copy this notes skeleton on paper BY HAND:
     -----------------------------------------
     ENGLISH - MY NOTES (day: ______)
     3 new words from this week:
     1. ______ = ______________________
     2. ______ = ______________________
     3. ______ = ______________________
     My question for the teacher:
     What does ______ mean?
     -----------------------------------------
  2. Complete it: choose 3 REAL words you know from
     Topics 11-15 and explain each in simple English
     (word = ...).
  3. Write one real question for your teacher.
  4. Take a photo and upload it.

Minimum requirements:
  - Skeleton copied completely, day filled in
  - 3 words + simple English explanations
  - The What does ... mean? question with a real word

RUBRIC:
  1. Notes complete, format followed        [ pass / not yet ]
  2. 3 real words, sensible explanations    [ pass / not yet ]
  3. Survival question correct              [ pass / not yet ]
  4. Handwriting readable (A1 bar)          [ pass / not yet ]
