==============================================================
TASILLA - A1 FINAL TESTS / WRITING PATH FINAL TEST
(STEP 27 OF 27 OF THE WRITING SKILL PATH)
==============================================================
Covers: Topics 1-20. Teacher-graded. Passing (teacher
approval) completes the Writing path and finalizes the
Writing skill score for the certificate.

DESIGN:
- 2 tasks in ONE session, mirroring the course's two
  writing worlds: a practical form/message task + a
  free personal text
- Task 1 typed; Task 2 HANDWRITTEN and uploaded (the
  certificate claims handwritten ability - the final
  test proves it one last time). If the upload feature
  is not live, both run typed (flag on certificate
  evidence).
- Teacher grades a 6-criteria rubric across both tasks
- XP: 30 on submit, +20 on approval

==============================================================
TASK 1 - PRACTICAL WRITING (typed, ~10 minutes)
==============================================================

Situation shown in app:
  You are sick today, and you have an English class at
  the school this afternoon. Write a message to your
  teacher (5-6 sentences):
  - say you are sick + ONE symptom (have + ...)
  - say what you can't do
  - one sentence with -ing: what is happening at home
    now
  - ask ONE question (about the homework, the next
    class, a word...)
  - say when the teacher sees you again (day - T8)

==============================================================
TASK 2 - PERSONAL TEXT (HANDWRITTEN + uploaded,
~20 minutes)
==============================================================

Choose ONE prompt card (the app offers all three):
  A) MY LIFE - who you are, family, home, work or
     study, one hobby
  B) A PERSON I LOVE - who + description + clothes +
     what they do every day + what they can do
  C) MY PERFECT SATURDAY - morning to night: weather,
     food, places, free time (careful: all in PRESENT
     tense - what you DO on your perfect Saturday!)

Requirements:
  - 10-12 sentences BY HAND on paper
  - Then photograph and upload

==============================================================
RUBRIC (6 criteria, across both tasks)
==============================================================
  1. Task 1: message complete - all 5
     content points                          [ pass / not yet ]
  2. Task 1: have/can't/-ing forms correct   [ pass / not yet ]
  3. Task 2: 10-12 sentences, on the chosen
     prompt, organized                       [ pass / not yet ]
  4. Task 2: grammar range - to be, have,
     present + -s, do-system or can, one
     time expression                         [ pass / not yet ]
  5. Vocabulary range: words from 8+
     different topics across both tasks      [ pass / not yet ]
  6. Spelling + handwriting readable
     throughout (A1 bar)                     [ pass / not yet ]

Approval bar: 5 of 6, criterion 6 mandatory.
Teacher action: Approve / Request redo of ONE task.

==============================================================
GRADING GUIDANCE FOR TEACHERS
==============================================================
- A1 exit writing = short, simple, correct-enough
  sentences with and/but/so. Do NOT expect paragraphs
  or connectors beyond these.
- Prompt C trap: students may slip toward future or
  past forms they picked up elsewhere. Course scope is
  present systems only - if a student writes correct
  future/past, do not penalize, but grade the A1
  systems (that is what the certificate claims).
- Spelling bar: readable and consistent; phonetic slips
  on hard words are A1-normal (beleave/believe), but
  course words (family, kitchen, Tuesday...) should
  be right.

==============================================================
PRODUCTION NOTES
