==============================================================
TASILLA - A1 CONTENT / TOPIC 18: CLOTHES & COLOURS
==============================================================
Unit 4 - Wider World | Difficulty dial: Topics 16-20
(10 new words, 4 options, inference questions, natural-pace
audio ~40s, reading texts 60-80 words, dictation 6-7 words,
60-second monologues, full recycling)

Grammar embedded: PRESENT CONTINUOUS with WEAR (G10: She is
wearing a red dress) + habit contrast (I wear a jacket
every winter) + THESE/THOSE recycled with plural clothes
(G4: these shoes, those jackets)
Can-do: Name clothes and colours; describe what people
are wearing
Recycles: T4 (describing people - colours were PROMISED
there and arrive now), T11 (shopping, prices, this/these),
T17 (continuous + seasons), T9 (prices)

TARGET LANGUAGE
Words (10): clothes, shirt, dress, shoes, jacket, wear,
            red, blue, black, white
(green and yellow appear in texts as passive exposure -
tested colours are the big four)
Phrases:    What are you wearing? / He is wearing a blue
            shirt. / I wear a jacket in winter. / What
            colour is it? / these shoes / my new dress
Structures: colour before noun: a RED dress, BLACK shoes
            (same slot as big/small - T4!)
            wear (habit) vs am/is/are wearing (now) -
            the G10 contrast on its most useful verb
NOTE: shoes is taught as a plural word (these shoes ARE) -
flag on intro card. trousers/pants NOT taught (plural
minefield; A1 bar covered without them).

==============================================================
LESSON 18.1 - VOCABULARY (auto-graded, 8 exercises, all tap)
==============================================================

Intro card:
  clothes = shirts, dresses, jackets - all of it!
  shirt = on your top  |  dress = one piece, top to legs
  shoes = on your feet (always TWO - shoes ARE!)
  jacket = warm, for cold days  |  wear = have clothes on
  colours: red | blue | black | white
  + phrase bank: What are you wearing? | What colour
    is it? | these shoes
  + grammar note: colour BEFORE the thing: a red dress,
    white shoes (like big table - T4!)

EX 1 - Word meaning (MC)
Q: They are on your feet, and they are always two: ___.
   a) shoes   [CORRECT]
   b) shirts
   c) jackets
   d) hats
Feedback: Shoes = feet, always a pair - these shoes ARE.

EX 2 - Word meaning (MC)
Q: Snow is ___. The night sky is ___.
   a) white / black   [CORRECT]
   b) black / white
   c) red / blue
   d) blue / red
Feedback: Snow (T17!) = white. Night = black.

EX 3 - Gap-fill (tap the missing word)
Q: It is winter and very cold - I need my ___!
   a) jacket   [CORRECT]
   b) dress
   c) juice
   d) shoes
Feedback: Jacket = the warm one, for winter (T17).

EX 4 - Gap-fill (tap the missing word)
Q: What do you ___ to work? - A white shirt, every day.
   a) wear   [CORRECT]
   b) eat
   c) buy
   d) watch
Feedback: Wear = have clothes on you.

EX 5 - Odd one out (MC)
Q: Which one is NOT clothes?
   a) shirt
   b) snow   [CORRECT]
   c) dress
   d) jacket
Feedback: Snow (T17) is weather - very cold on your
head, and NOT clothes!

EX 6 - Dialogue completion (MC)
Q: A: What colour is your new dress?  B: ___
   a) It is red - my favorite colour!   [CORRECT]
   b) It is forty dollars.
   c) I am wearing it now.
   d) It is on the table.
Feedback: What COLOUR...? -> answer with a colour!

EX 7 - GRAMMAR: wearing now (MC)
Q: Look at Vado! He ___ a big red hat right now!
   a) is wearing   [CORRECT]
   b) wears
   c) wearing
   d) wear
Feedback: Right now -> IS WEARING (G10, same as T17!).

EX 8 - GRAMMAR: the contrast + word order (MC)
Q: Every day I ___ black shoes. Today I ___ my new
white shoes!
   a) wear / am wearing   [CORRECT]
   b) am wearing / wear
   c) wears / wearing
   d) wearing / wear
Feedback: Every day = habit -> wear. Today, on me now ->
am wearing.

Pass: 70% (6 of 8). XP: 10.

LESSON 18.3 - READING (auto-graded, 2 texts, 3 questions each)
==============================================================

TEXT A (63 words) - Clothes shop flyer format:
---------------------------------------
  MODA MIA - NEW CLOTHES THIS WEEK!
  White shirts ......... twenty dollars
  Red dresses .......... forty dollars
  Black shoes .......... thirty-five dollars
  Winter jackets ....... fifty dollars
  * THIS WEEK ONLY: two white shirts for thirty dollars!
  Open Monday to Saturday, nine to seven.
  We are next to the bank - come and see the new
  colours!
---------------------------------------

EX 1 - Gist (MC)
Q: What is this text?
   a) A clothes shop flyer   [CORRECT]
   b) A weather report
   c) A party invitation
   d) A postcard

EX 2 - Detail: price (MC)
Q: How much are the black shoes?
   a) Thirty-five dollars   [CORRECT]
   b) Fifty dollars
   c) Twenty dollars
   d) Forty dollars

EX 3 - INFERENCE (MC)
Q: It is very cold in your city. Which thing in this
shop is for you?
   a) A winter jacket   [CORRECT]
   b) A red dress
   c) Two white shirts
   d) Black shoes
Feedback: Cold (T17!) -> the warm one -> the winter
jacket.

TEXT B (68 words) - Chat messages format:
---------------------------------------
  Bia:  Rafa, help me! The party is at eight, and I am
        looking at my clothes NOW. Blue dress or white
        shirt?
  Rafa: Your blue dress is super!
  Bia:  Hmm... no. Blue is not my colour.
  Rafa: OK: the white shirt, and your new black shoes!
  Bia:  Yes!! Perfect. And you? What are you wearing?
  Rafa: My green jacket, of course. I love it!
---------------------------------------

EX 4 - Gist (MC)
Q: What is this chat about?
   a) Bia chooses clothes for a party tonight   [CORRECT]
   b) Bia buys a dress in a shop
   c) The weather at a party
   d) Rafa's new shoes

EX 5 - Detail (MC)
Q: What does Bia wear in the end?
   a) The white shirt and black shoes   [CORRECT]
   b) The blue dress
   c) A green jacket
   d) The text does not say

EX 6 - Grammar in context (MC)
Q: Bia asks: What ___ you ___? (about clothes, right
now/tonight)
   a) are / wearing   [CORRECT]
   b) do / wear
   c) is / wearing
   d) are / wear
Feedback: The party is NOW-tonight, clothes on the body
-> What ARE you WEARING?

Pass: 70% (5 of 6). XP: 10.

LESSON 18.4 - SPEAKING (teacher-graded, 1 recorded task)
==============================================================

PROMPT (no example - free stage; 60-second monologue):
  Record a 60-second monologue: CLOTHES AND COLOURS.
  Cover:
  1. What you are wearing RIGHT NOW - with colours
     (I am wearing...)
  2. What you wear every day to work/school - the habit
     (I wear...)
  3. One person: what he/she is wearing now, or wears
     every day (T4 + T3: describe a real person!)
  4. Your favorite colour + one thing you have in that
     colour

Max recording: 75 seconds (target: fill 60).
Tip: Look at yourself - real clothes, real colours.
Easy to speak about what you see!

RUBRIC:
  1. Fills ~60s, organized                  [ pass / not yet ]
  2. am/is wearing vs wear used correctly   [ pass / not yet ]
  3. Colour + clothes word order correct    [ pass / not yet ]
  4. Understandable pronunciation           [ pass / not yet ]

XP: 15 on submit, +10 on approval.
