LESSON 5.1 - VOCABULARY (auto-graded, 8 exercises, all tap)
==============================================================

Intro card:
  happy = you smile!  |  sad = you are not happy
  tired = you need sleep  |  hungry = you need food
  thirsty = you need water  |  cold = brrr!
  + phrase bank: How are you? | Are you tired? |
    Yes, I am / No, I am not

EX 1 - Word meaning (MC)
Q: You need food. You are ___.
   a) hungry   [CORRECT]
   b) thirsty
   c) happy
Feedback: Hungry = you need food. Thirsty = you need water.

EX 2 - Word meaning (MC)
Q: It is 11 at night and you need sleep. You are ___.
   a) tired   [CORRECT]
   b) cold
   c) sad
Feedback: Tired = you need sleep.

EX 3 - Gap-fill (tap the missing word)
Q: Water, please! I am very ___.
   a) thirsty   [CORRECT]
   b) hungry
   c) tall
Feedback: Thirsty = you need water.

EX 4 - Gap-fill (tap the missing word)
Q: Brrr! I am very ___ today.
   a) cold   [CORRECT]
   b) happy
   c) nice
Feedback: Cold = brrr! You need a warm room.

EX 5 - Odd one out (MC)
Q: Which word is NOT a feeling?
   a) sad
   b) morning   [CORRECT]
   c) tired
Feedback: Morning is a time of day (Topic 1) - not a feeling.

EX 6 - Dialogue completion (MC)
Q: A: How are you?  B: ___
   a) I am tired but happy.   [CORRECT]
   b) I am from Recife.
   c) My name is Ana.
Feedback: How are you? asks about feelings - answer with I am +
feeling.

EX 7 - GRAMMAR: to be, new persons (MC)
Q: My sister and I ___ hungry. My parents ___ tired.
   a) are / are   [CORRECT]
   b) is / are
   c) am / is
Feedback: My sister and I = WE -> are. My parents = THEY -> are.

EX 8 - GRAMMAR: question + short answer (MC)
Q: A: ___ you cold?  B: No, I ___ not.
   a) Are / am   [CORRECT]
   b) Is / are
   c) Are / is
Feedback: Are you...? -> Yes, I am / No, I am not.

Pass: 70% (6 of 8). XP: 10.

LESSON 5.4 - SPEAKING (teacher-graded, 1 recorded task)
==============================================================

PROMPT:
  Record yourself. Talk about feelings today.
  Say 4 things:
  1. A greeting + how you are (Hello! I am fine... - T1)
  2. TWO feeling words about you today (I am ... and ...)
  3. One family member or friend: how is he/she today?
     (My mother is... - T3)
  4. Ask ME a question: Are you...?
  Example: Hi! I am fine. I am happy but a little tired.
  My father is hungry! And you? Are you tired?

Max recording: 45 seconds.
Tip: Think: how are you REALLY today? Use true feelings!

RUBRIC:
  1. Uses 2+ feeling words about self       [ pass / not yet ]
  2. He/She is + feeling for another person [ pass / not yet ]
  3. Asks Are you...? question              [ pass / not yet ]
  4. Understandable pronunciation           [ pass / not yet ]

XP: 15 on submit, +10 on approval.
