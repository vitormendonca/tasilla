==============================================================
TASILLA - A1 CONTENT / TOPIC 13: TRAVEL & TRANSPORT
==============================================================
Unit 3 - Out in the World | Difficulty dial: Topics 11-15
(8 new words, 4 options, 'not stated' live, audio ~35s
dialogues, reading texts 50-60 words, dictation 6 words,
free-write)

Grammar embedded: BY + transport (go by bus - no article) +
ticket-office requests with CAN (G11 recycled) + What time
is...? (T8 recycled with timetables)
Can-do: Name transport, buy tickets, handle simple travel
situations
Recycles: T8 (times, days), T9 (prices), T10-11 (Can I
have..., please), T12 (station, street, directions), T7
(go, 3rd person -s)

TARGET LANGUAGE
Words (8): bus, train, car, taxi, plane, ticket, airport,
           travel
Phrases:   I go to work by bus. / A ticket to Olinda,
           please. / What time is the bus? / The bus is at
           ten thirty. / Can I have two tickets, please?
Structures: by + transport, NO article (by bus - not by
            a bus)
            travel as verb: I travel / she travels (-s)
NOTE: NO future forms - trips are talked about with to be
+ schedule times (My bus is on Saturday at seven) and
simple present. 'I am going to...' is on the A2 excluded
list. walk / on foot NOT taught (margin) - transport only.

==============================================================
LESSON 13.1 - VOCABULARY (auto-graded, 8 exercises, all tap)
==============================================================

Intro card:
  bus = big, many people, in the city
  train = long, on rails, from the station
  car = your family drives it  |  taxi = a car you pay for
  plane = it flies! (airport)
  ticket = your paper to go in (bus, train, plane)
  airport = planes go from there
  travel = go to another city or country
  + phrase bank: by bus / by train / by car |
    A ticket to..., please | What time is the bus?

EX 1 - Word meaning (MC)
Q: It flies, and it is at the airport. It is a ___.
   a) plane   [CORRECT]
   b) train
   c) taxi
   d) bus
Feedback: Plane = it flies. Train = on rails.

EX 2 - Word meaning (MC)
Q: A car with a driver - you pay at the end. It is a ___.
   a) taxi   [CORRECT]
   b) bus
   c) ticket
   d) plane
Feedback: Taxi = a car you pay for.

EX 3 - Gap-fill (tap the missing word)
Q: Your ___ please! - Here you are. Seat twelve.
   a) ticket   [CORRECT]
   b) airport
   c) menu
   d) bag
Feedback: Ticket = your paper for the bus, train or plane.

EX 4 - Gap-fill (tap the missing word)
Q: In July we ___ to Portugal. My mother's family is there!
   a) travel   [CORRECT]
   b) sleep
   c) turn
   d) buy
Feedback: Travel = go to another city or country.

EX 5 - Odd one out (MC)
Q: Which one is NOT transport?
   a) bus
   b) kitchen   [CORRECT]
   c) train
   d) taxi
Feedback: Kitchen is a room (Topic 6)!

EX 6 - Dialogue completion (MC)
Q: Ticket office: Good morning! Can I help you?  You: ___
   a) A ticket to Recife, please. What time is the bus?
      [CORRECT]
   b) Turn left at the park.
   c) I have long hair.
   d) There is a table in the kitchen.
Feedback: At the ticket office: say WHERE + ask WHEN.

EX 7 - GRAMMAR: by + transport (MC)
Q: I go to work ___ bus. My father goes ___ car.
   a) by / by   [CORRECT]
   b) by a / by a
   c) in / on
   d) on / at
Feedback: BY bus, BY car, BY train - no a, no the!

EX 8 - GRAMMAR: travel + -s (MC)
Q: I ___ by train. My sister ___ by plane - expensive!
   a) travel / travels   [CORRECT]
   b) travels / travel
   c) travel / travel
   d) travels / travels
Feedback: I travel. She travelS (T7 rule).

Pass: 70% (6 of 8). XP: 10.

LESSON 13.2 - LISTENING (auto-graded, 1 audio + 5 exercises)
==============================================================

AUDIO SCRIPT (to record - 2 speakers, slow-natural,
file: audio/a1/t13_listening_two_tickets_please.mp3, ~35 s):

  Clerk: Good morning! Can I help you?
  Malu:  Yes! A ticket to Olinda, please. What time is

  Clerk: Seven dollars.

PRODUCTION NOTES
==============================================================
- 1 audio to record (~35s, 2 speakers) + 1 review line (~12s)
- STRICT no-future fence: trips use to be + time (My bus
  is on Saturday) and simple present (I travel on
  Saturday) - NEVER going to / will
- present, travelers, seat, driver, app = passive exposure
- Ticket math (7 -> 14) is SAID in the audio - the item
  never requires the student to calculate
- Recycling check: Can I have (T10/11), station + next to
  (T12), times incl. thirty (T8), prices + danger pairs
  (T9), goes/likes -s (T7)
