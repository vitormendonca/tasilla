==============================================================
TASILLA - A1 CONTENT / TOPIC 9: NUMBERS, PRICES & MONEY
==============================================================
Unit 2 - Everyday Life | Difficulty dial: Topics 6-10
(8 new words, 3 options, audio 4-5 sentences ~30s,
reading texts 40-50 words, T/F in reading, dictation 5-6
words, systematic recycling, scaffold fading)

Grammar embedded: HOW MUCH IS / HOW MUCH ARE (singular vs
plural - extends the T6 is/are agreement work) + plural -s
recycling (G3). Numbers 13-100 taught as a SET on the intro
card (precedent: T2 alphabet, T8 days).
Can-do: Count, understand prices, handle money situations
Recycles: T6 (table, chair, bed as things with prices),
T8 (numbers as clock digits), T2 (single-digit numbers),
T7 (buy/pay join the verb set)

TARGET LANGUAGE
Words (8): money, price, dollar, cent, cheap, expensive,
           buy, pay
Set on intro card: numbers 13-100 (thirteen... twenty,
           thirty... one hundred), with the THIRTEEN vs
           THIRTY danger pairs flagged (13/30, 14/40...)
Phrases:   How much is it? / It is ninety dollars. /
           How much are the chairs? / That is expensive! /
           Very cheap! / I pay.
Structures: How much IS + singular / How much ARE + plural
            plural -s: one chair -> four chairs
NOTE: currency word localizable at production time (dollars
here; reais/euros per market - same audio structure).
shopping (the activity, shops, sizes) is Topic 11 - here
the focus is numbers and price language only.

==============================================================
LESSON 9.1 - VOCABULARY (auto-graded, 8 exercises, all tap)
==============================================================

Intro card:
  money = dollars and cents!  |  price = 90 dollars is a price
  dollar = 1.00  |  cent = 0.01 (100 cents = 1 dollar)
  cheap = a small price :)  |  expensive = a big price!
  buy = get a thing with money  |  pay = give the money
  + set: numbers 13-100 with audio; danger pairs 13/30,
    14/40, 15/50 flagged
  + phrase bank: How much is it? | How much are they? |
    That is expensive!

EX 1 - Word meaning (MC)
Q: Dollars and cents are ___.
   a) money   [CORRECT]
   b) days
   c) rooms
Feedback: Money = dollars and cents.

EX 2 - Word meaning (MC)
Q: The table is 500 dollars! It is very ___.
   a) expensive   [CORRECT]
   b) cheap
   c) hungry
Feedback: Expensive = a big price. Cheap = a small price.

EX 3 - Gap-fill (tap the missing word)
Q: I want this book. I ___ it with my money.
   a) buy   [CORRECT]
   b) sleep
   c) study
Feedback: Buy = get a thing with money.

EX 4 - Gap-fill (tap the missing word)
Q: The price is ten dollars. I ___ with my card.
   a) pay   [CORRECT]
   b) drink
   c) get up
Feedback: Pay = give the money.

EX 5 - Number recognition (MC)
Q: 30 = ___
   a) thirty   [CORRECT]
   b) thirteen
   c) three
Feedback: thirTEEN = 13. thirTY = 30. Listen for the end!

EX 6 - Dialogue completion (MC)
Q: A: How much is the chair?  B: ___
   a) It is fifteen dollars.   [CORRECT]
   b) It is on Monday.
   c) There are two chairs.
Feedback: How much...? asks the PRICE -> answer in dollars.

EX 7 - GRAMMAR: How much is / are (MC)
Q: How much ___ the bed? How much ___ the chairs?
   a) is / are   [CORRECT]
   b) are / is
   c) is / is
Feedback: One bed -> IS. Two or more chairs -> ARE.

EX 8 - GRAMMAR: plural -s (MC)
Q: One chair is cheap. Four ___ are expensive!
   a) chairs   [CORRECT]
   b) chair
   c) chair's
Feedback: 2, 3, 4... -> add -s. (chair's = of the chair, T3!)

Pass: 70% (6 of 8). XP: 10.

LESSON 9.2 - LISTENING (auto-graded, 1 audio + 5 exercises)
==============================================================

AUDIO SCRIPT (to record - 2 speakers, slow, clear pauses,
file: audio/a1/t09_listening_how_much_is_it.mp3, ~30 sec):

  Man:   Good morning! How much is this table?
  Woman: It is ninety dollars.
  Man:   Ninety?! That is expensive! And how much are
         the chairs?
  Woman: The chairs are thirteen dollars.
  Man:   Thirteen? Very cheap! Four chairs, please. I pay
         with my card.

Max plays: 3.

EX 1 - Gist (MC)
Q: Where is this conversation?
   a) In a shop   [CORRECT]
   b) In a school
   c) At a birthday party

EX 2 - Detail: number (MC)
Q: How much is the table?
   a) Ninety dollars   [CORRECT]
   b) Nineteen dollars
   c) Nine dollars

EX 3 - Detail: number danger pair (MC)
Q: How much are the chairs?
   a) Thirteen dollars   [CORRECT]
   b) Thirty dollars
   c) Three dollars
Feedback: thirTEEN = 13. Listen for the end of the word!

EX 4 - True / False (MC)
Q: The table is cheap. True or false?
   a) True
   b) False   [CORRECT]  (ninety dollars - expensive!)

EX 5 - DICTATION (type what you hear - 5 words)
Audio repeats: The chairs are thirteen dollars.
Correct answer: The chairs are thirteen dollars
(accept case-insensitive, ignore final punctuation;
accept 13 for thirteen)

Pass: 70% (4 of 5). XP: 10.

EX 1 - Gist (MC)

   a) A shop flyer with prices   [CORRECT]
   b) A birthday invitation
   c) A week schedule

EX 2 - Detail: number (MC)
Q: How much is the bed?
   a) Eighty dollars   [CORRECT]
   b) Eighteen dollars
   c) Forty dollars

EX 3 - True / False (MC)
Q: The chair is expensive. True or false?
   a) True
   b) False   [CORRECT]  (fifteen dollars - very cheap!)

TEXT B (48 words) - Chat messages format:
---------------------------------------
  Ravi: Mama, I need money for school, please!
  Mama: Why, Ravi?
  Ravi: My new English book is twenty dollars.
  Mama: Twenty? OK, that is not expensive. And your
        brother's book?
  Ravi: His book is thirty dollars!
  Mama: Thirty?! OK, OK. I pay for the two books.
---------------------------------------

EX 4 - Gist (MC)
Q: What does Ravi want?
   a) Money for school books   [CORRECT]
   b) A new table
   c) A birthday party

EX 5 - Detail: number danger pair (MC)
Q: How much is Ravi's book?
   a) Twenty dollars   [CORRECT]
   b) Thirty dollars
   c) Twelve dollars
Feedback: Ravi's book = 20. His BROTHER'S book = 30 (T3 's!).

EX 6 - Grammar in context (MC)
Q: Mama says: I ___ for the two books.
   a) pay   [CORRECT]
   b) pays
   c) buy
Feedback: I pay (no -s with I). Pay = give the money.

Pass: 70% (5 of 6). XP: 10.

LESSON 9.4 - SPEAKING (teacher-graded, 1 recorded task)
==============================================================

PROMPT (no example - free stage):
  Record yourself. You are in a shop (in your head!).
  Say 4 things:
  1. Ask the price of one thing: How much is the...?
  2. Answer with a price: It is ... dollars.
  3. Say cheap or expensive: That is...!
  4. Ask about MORE than one thing: How much are the...?
     (bed, table, chairs, books, phone...)

Max recording: 60 seconds.
Tip: Say the numbers slowly and clearly - thirTEEN or
thirTY? Make it easy to hear.

RUBRIC:
  1. How much is/are matches sing./plural   [ pass / not yet ]
  2. Prices said with clear numbers         [ pass / not yet ]
  3. cheap/expensive used correctly         [ pass / not yet ]
  4. Understandable pronunciation           [ pass / not yet ]

XP: 15 on submit, +10 on approval.

==============================================================
LESSON 9.5 - WRITING (teacher-graded, MODE B - HANDWRITTEN,
FREE-WRITE)
==============================================================

FOURTH PAPER TASK. Student writes BY HAND on paper, then
uploads a photo / scan / PDF.

TASK: Write a shopping list with prices by hand.

Instructions shown in app:
  1. On paper, write a list of 5 things you buy (things
     you know in English: book, table, chair, phone...).
  2. Write a price for each thing.
  3. For TWO things, write the price in WORDS
     (twenty dollars - not 20).
  4. At the end, write one sentence: The ... is cheap! or
     The ... is expensive!
  5. Take a photo and upload it.

Minimum requirements:
  - 5 items, each with a price
  - 2 prices written out in words, spelled correctly
  - 1 cheap/expensive sentence

RUBRIC:
  1. 5 items with prices, list format       [ pass / not yet ]
  2. 2 number words spelled correctly       [ pass / not yet ]
  3. cheap/expensive sentence correct       [ pass / not yet ]
  4. Handwriting readable (A1 bar)          [ pass / not yet ]

Teacher view: opens image/PDF + rubric side by side.
Approve / Request redo (+ feedback).
XP: 15 on submit, +10 on approval.

DEPENDENCY NOTE: requires writing-upload feature. Until
built, runs temporarily as typed Mode A with the same
list format.
