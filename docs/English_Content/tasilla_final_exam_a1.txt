==============================================================
TASILLA - A1 FINAL EXAM (SUPERVISED, MULTI-FORMAT)
THE LAST ROADMAP STEP BEFORE THE CERTIFICATE
==============================================================
Covers: the full course - Topics 1-20, G1-G12.
Eligibility: Checkpoints 1-4 passed, Portfolios 1-2
approved, all five skill-path Final Tests passed.

CERTIFICATION STANDARD (locked):
- Multi-format: dictation + gap-fill + production, plus
  listening and reading comprehension
- PASS = overall score >= 75% AND every section >= 65%
- Supervised: teacher present (in person or live video),
  one sitting, ~60 minutes
- One attempt per sitting; retake after 7+ days; after
  3 fails, teacher review meeting required
- No section may be skipped. XP: 100 on pass.

SECTIONS & WEIGHTS (equal - matches the 4-skills-gated
philosophy, with Use of English as the fifth column):
  A Listening (incl. 2 dictations)  - 10 items -> %
  B Reading                         - 10 items -> %
  C Use of English (gap-fill)       - 10 items -> %
  D Writing (production)            - rubric /10 -> %
  E Speaking (production)           - rubric /10 -> %
  OVERALL = average of the five section percentages.

==============================================================
SECTION A - LISTENING (10 items; audio in 2 scenes)
==============================================================

AUDIO SCRIPT (to record - 3 voices,
file: audio/a1/finalexam_listening.mp3, ~65 sec.
Scene 1 moderate pace, Scene 2 natural pace):

  SCENE 1 (monologue):
  Hello! I am Zara. Z - A - R - A. I am 26 years old,
  and I am from Faro. I am a teacher - I work at a
  small school next to the park. Every day I go to
  work by bus, at seven thirty.

  SCENE 2 (dialogue, in a cafe):
  Man:   Good morning! Can I have a coffee and bread,
         please? How much is it?
  Woman: Three dollars. ... Oh, look - it is raining
         again!
  Man:   Yes, big rain! And my son is sick at home -
         he has a headache. I need his medicine. The
         pharmacy is next to the bank, yes?
  Woman: Yes, very near. Get well to him! Here - your
         coffee and bread.

Max plays: 2 per scene.

EX 1 - What is Zara's job?
   a) Teacher [CORRECT]  b) Doctor  c) Student
   d) Office worker
EX 2 - How does she spell her name?
   a) Z-A-R-A [CORRECT]  b) S-A-R-A  c) Z-A-R-R-A
   d) Z-E-R-A
EX 3 - How does Zara go to work, and when?
   a) By bus at seven thirty [CORRECT]
   b) By train at seven
   c) By car at nine thirty
   d) The audio does not say
EX 4 - Where is her school?
   a) Next to the park [CORRECT]  b) Next to the bank
   c) At the station  d) In Faro center
EX 5 - What does the man order, and how much is it?
   a) Coffee and bread - three dollars [CORRECT]
   b) Tea and bread - three dollars
   c) Coffee and an apple - thirteen dollars
   d) The audio does not say
EX 6 - What is the weather like?
   a) It is raining [CORRECT]  b) It is snowing
   c) It is hot and sunny  d) The audio does not say
EX 7 - What is wrong at home, and what does the man
   need?
   a) His son has a headache; he needs medicine from
      the pharmacy   [CORRECT]
   b) His son is cold; he needs a jacket
   c) The man has a headache; he needs a doctor
   d) The audio does not say
EX 8 - NOT STATED: How old is the man's son?
   a) 6  b) 10  c) 16
   d) The audio does not say [CORRECT]
EX 9 - DICTATION 1 (type - 6 words)
   Audio repeats: I go to work by bus.
   Correct: I go to work by bus
EX 10 - DICTATION 2 (type - 4 words)
   Audio repeats: He has a headache.
   Correct: He has a headache
(dictations: case-insensitive, ignore punctuation)

Section A score: /10 -> %. Section pass line: 6.5 -> in
practice 7 of 10.

SECTION C - USE OF ENGLISH (10 gap-fill items, tap)
==============================================================

EX 1 - I ___ 25 years old, and my parents ___ from
Recife.
   a) am / are [CORRECT]  b) is / are  c) am / is
   d) are / am
EX 2 - This is my ___ car. (the car of my father)
   a) father's [CORRECT]  b) fathers  c) father
   d) father is
EX 3 - ___ two bedrooms, and ___ a big kitchen.
   a) There are / there is [CORRECT]
   b) There is / there are
   c) They are / it is
   d) There are / there are
EX 4 - She ___ up at seven, and she ___ to work by
train.
   a) gets / goes [CORRECT]  b) get / go
   c) gets / gos  d) get / goes
EX 5 - The party is ___ Saturday ___ four o'clock.
   a) on / at [CORRECT]  b) at / on  c) in / in
   d) on / in
EX 6 - Can I have ___ apple and ___ coffee, please?
   a) an / a [CORRECT]  b) a / an  c) a / a
   d) an / an
EX 7 - How much are ___ shoes here? And ___ jacket
there?
   a) these / that [CORRECT]  b) this / those
   c) these / these  d) that / this
EX 8 - My brother ___ speak English, but he ___ speak
Italian.
   a) can / can't [CORRECT]  b) cans / can't
   c) can / doesn't can  d) can / no can
EX 9 - ___ you like films? - No, I ___.
   a) Do / don't [CORRECT]  b) Does / doesn't
   c) Are / am not  d) Do / doesn't
EX 10 - It ___ every winter. Look - it ___ right now!
   a) rains / is raining [CORRECT]
   b) is raining / rains
   c) rain / rains
   d) rains / raining

==============================================================
SECTION D - WRITING (production, supervised, ~15 min)
==============================================================

TASK (typed in app under supervision; if Mode B
infrastructure is live, teacher MAY set it as
handwritten + immediate upload):

  Write about YOUR LIFE for a person who does not know
  you. 10 sentences minimum.
  Must include:
  1. Name, age, city (T1-2)
  2. One family member or friend + description (T3-4)
  3. Your home (there is/are) (T6)
  4. Your day: 2 routine verbs + one time expression
     (T7-8)
  5. Job or study + one can sentence (T14)
  6. One hobby + one don't/doesn't sentence (T16)
  7. The weather right now, with -ing (T17)

SCORING RUBRIC (/10):
  Content: all 7 points present ........... 0-4 points
  Grammar: A1 systems correct
  (to be, have, -s, do-system, -ing) ...... 0-3 points
  Vocabulary: range across topics ......... 0-2 points
  Spelling: readable, course words right .. 0-1 point

Section D score: points x 10 -> %. Section pass line:
6.5/10.

==============================================================
SECTION E - SPEAKING (production, supervised, ~8 min)
==============================================================

Run live with the teacher (in person / call) or
recorded under supervision:

PART 1 - INTERVIEW (teacher asks, student answers in
full sentences):
  1. What is your name? Can you spell your first name?
  2. Where are you from, and how old are you?
  3. Tell me about your family.
  4. What do you do every day?
  5. What is the weather like today?
  6. Do you like music? What is your hobby?

PART 2 - MONOLOGUE (student speaks 60 seconds; teacher
picks ONE):
  A) Your home and your city
  B) A person you love
  C) Your perfect Saturday

SCORING RUBRIC (/10):
  Interview: 6 answers, full sentences .... 0-3 points
  Monologue: 60s, organized, on topic ..... 0-3 points
  Grammar range across both parts ......... 0-2 points
  Pronunciation: understandable (A1) ...... 0-2 points

Section E score: points x 10 -> %. Section pass line:
6.5/10.

==============================================================
FINAL SCORING & CERTIFICATE
==============================================================
OVERALL = (A% + B% + C% + D% + E%) / 5

PASS RULES (certification standard):
  - OVERALL >= 75%
  - AND every single section >= 65%
  (a 90% reader who scores 50% in speaking does NOT
  pass - no averaging across the gate)

ON PASS:
  - Certificate issued: CEFR A1, with the can-do list
    (Topics 1-20), skill scores, checkpoint record,
    portfolio evidence links, exam date + supervisor
  - Handwriting evidence: lesson tasks 2/5/8/9/12/15/
    17/20 referenced on the certificate trail

ON FAIL:
  - Section report to student + teacher: which
    section(s) under 65% / overall under 75%
  - Targeted revision plan auto-suggested: the failing
    section's skill path Review 4-6 + relevant mixed
    reviews
  - Retake after 7+ days (new exam form: production
    prompts rotate, audio/text variants B recorded at
    production time)
