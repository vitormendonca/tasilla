==============================================================
PART A - SPEAKING PORTFOLIO 2: "A DAY IN MY WORLD"
==============================================================

PROMPT (shown to student):
  Record 90-120 seconds - your longest recording of the
  course. Tell the story of a REAL day in your life,
  morning to night, TODAY or a typical day.
  Cover:
  1. The start: the day, the weather right now, how you
     are (T8, T17, T5)
  2. Your morning: routine + transport (T7, T13)
  3. Your work or study + one thing you can do (T14)
  4. Out in the world: a shop, cafe or place in town -
     with one price or direction (T9-12)
  5. Your evening: free time, family, your animal if
     you have one (T16, T3, T20)
  6. What you are wearing while you record this! (T18)
  Speak freely - this is YOUR day, not a list.

Max recording: 120 seconds (target: fill 90+).
Tip: Record it like a voice message to a friend who
moved away and misses your life.

RUBRIC (6 criteria):
  1. Covers 5+ of the 6 content areas       [ pass / not yet ]
  2. Simple present + 3rd person -s solid   [ pass / not yet ]
  3. Continuous used correctly for 'now'    [ pass / not yet ]
  4. do/does/don't/doesn't + can correct    [ pass / not yet ]
  5. Connected speech: and/but/so, not a
     word list                              [ pass / not yet ]
  6. Understandable throughout (A1 bar)     [ pass / not yet ]

Approval bar: 5 of 6 criteria pass, criterion 6
mandatory.

==============================================================
PART B - WRITING PORTFOLIO 2: "A LETTER TO MY NEXT TEACHER"
==============================================================

PROMPT (typed in app, NO model):
  Your next English teacher (A2 course!) wants to know
  you before day one. Write a letter: 10-12 sentences.
  Include:
  - Who you are: name, age, city, job or studies
    (T1-2, T14)
  - Your people: one family member or friend, with a
    description (T3-4, T18 colours welcome)
  - Your life: home + one routine with times (T6-8)
  - Your likes: hobby + one thing you don't like (T16)
  - Right now: the weather and what you are doing as
    you write (T17)
  - One question for the new teacher (Do you...? /
    Can you...?)

Minimum requirements:
  - 10-12 sentences, letter shape (greeting + sign-off)
  - At least one: 's possessive, there is/are, can,
    don't/doesn't, present continuous
  - One question to the reader

RUBRIC (6 criteria):
  1. All six content areas present          [ pass / not yet ]
  2. to be / have / there is systems solid  [ pass / not yet ]
  3. Simple present + -s + do-system solid  [ pass / not yet ]
  4. Continuous correct for 'now'           [ pass / not yet ]
  5. Letter format + question to reader     [ pass / not yet ]
  6. Spelling readable throughout (A1 bar)  [ pass / not yet ]

Approval bar: 5 of 6 criteria pass, criterion 6
mandatory.
