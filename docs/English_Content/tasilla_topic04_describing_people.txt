==============================================================
TASILLA - A1 CONTENT / TOPIC 4: DESCRIBING PEOPLE
==============================================================
Unit 1 - Foundation | Difficulty dial: Topics 1-5
(6 new words, 3 options, slow audio 3-4 sentences,
reading texts 30-40 words, dictation 4-5 words)

Grammar embedded: TO BE + adjective (He is tall) +
HAVE / HAS for features (She has long hair)
Consolidates: full is/are system from T1-3 (I am, you are,
he/she is)
Can-do: Describe a person's appearance and personality simply
Recycles: T3 (family words, his/her), T2 (age), T1 (to be)

TARGET LANGUAGE
Words (6): tall, short, long, hair, eyes, nice
Phrases:   He is tall. / She is short. / I have long hair. /
           She has big eyes. / He is very nice. /
           What does she look like?
Structures: to be + adjective (is tall / is nice)
            have vs has (I/you HAVE - he/she HAS)
NOTE: colors NOT taught here (that is Topic 18). Descriptions
use size/length only: tall, short, long, big.

==============================================================
LESSON 4.1 - VOCABULARY (auto-graded, 8 exercises, all tap)
==============================================================

Intro card:
  tall = a big person (up!)  |  short = a small person
  long = long hair (down to the back)
  hair = on your head  |  eyes = you see with them
  nice = a good, friendly person
  + phrase bank: He is tall | She has long hair |
    What does he look like?

EX 1 - Word meaning (MC)
Q: A basketball player is usually ___.
   a) tall   [CORRECT]
   b) short
   c) long
Feedback: Tall = big up! People are tall. Hair is long.

EX 2 - Word meaning (MC)
Q: You see with your ___.
   a) eyes   [CORRECT]
   b) hair
   c) family
Feedback: Eyes = for seeing. Hair = on your head.

EX 3 - Gap-fill (tap the missing word)
Q: My sister has very ___ hair - down to her back!
   a) long   [CORRECT]
   b) tall
   c) nice
Feedback: Hair is LONG or SHORT. People are TALL or SHORT.

EX 4 - Gap-fill (tap the missing word)
Q: My teacher is very ___. I like her!
   a) nice   [CORRECT]
   b) long
   c) eyes
Feedback: Nice = good and friendly.

EX 5 - Odd one out (MC)
Q: Which word is NOT for describing people?
   a) tall
   b) email   [CORRECT]
   c) short
Feedback: Email is from Topic 2 - not a description word.

EX 6 - Dialogue completion (MC)
Q: A: What does your brother look like?  B: ___
   a) He is tall and he has short hair.   [CORRECT]
   b) He is from Brazil.
   c) His name is Leo.
Feedback: What does he LOOK LIKE? asks about appearance.

EX 7 - GRAMMAR: to be + adjective (MC)
Q: My mother ___ short. My parents ___ very nice.
   a) is / are   [CORRECT]
   b) are / is
   c) am / are
Feedback: She IS (one person). They ARE (two or more).

EX 8 - GRAMMAR: have / has (MC)
Q: I ___ short hair. My sister ___ long hair.
   a) have / has   [CORRECT]
   b) has / have
   c) has / has
Feedback: I/you HAVE. He/she HAS.

Pass: 70% (6 of 8). XP: 10.

==============================================================
LESSON 4.4 - SPEAKING (teacher-graded, 1 recorded task)
==============================================================

PROMPT:
  Record yourself. Describe ONE person you know well
  (family or friend).
  Say 4 things:
  1. Who they are + name (T3: This is my... His/Her name is...)
  2. Tall or short? (He/She is...)
  3. Their hair or eyes (He/She has...)
  4. Their personality (He/She is very nice / funny...)
  Example: This is my friend Bruno. He is tall. He has short
  hair. He is very nice.

Max recording: 45 seconds.
Tip: Look at a photo of the person while you speak!

RUBRIC:
  1. Identifies person + name (T3 recycle)  [ pass / not yet ]
  2. Uses is + adjective correctly          [ pass / not yet ]
  3. Uses has for hair/eyes correctly       [ pass / not yet ]
  4. Understandable pronunciation           [ pass / not yet ]

XP: 15 on submit, +10 on approval.

==============================================================
LESSON 4.5 - WRITING (teacher-graded, Mode A typed, SCAFFOLDED)
==============================================================

STEP 1 - MODEL (student reads):
  My best friend is Rafa.
  He is short and he is 20 years old.
  He has short hair and big eyes.
  He is very nice and funny.

STEP 2 - GAP-FILL the model (tap: has / is / nice / short):
  My best friend ___ Rafa.        -> is
  He is ___ and he is 20.         -> short
  He ___ short hair.              -> has
  He is very ___ and funny.       -> nice
  (warm-up, auto-checked, not scored)

STEP 3 - WRITE YOUR OWN VERSION (typed, teacher-graded):
Prompt: Describe a person you like (family or friend).
Minimum requirements:
  - 4 sentences
  - is + adjective at least twice (tall/short/nice...)
  - has + hair or eyes at least once
  - The person's name and who they are (T3)

RUBRIC:
  1. 4 sentences about a real person        [ pass / not yet ]
  2. is + adjective used correctly          [ pass / not yet ]
  3. have/has used correctly                [ pass / not yet ]
  4. Readable spelling (A1 bar)             [ pass / not yet ]

XP: 15 on submit, +10 on approval.

KINDNESS NOTE for teachers: descriptions stay positive and
neutral (tall, short, long hair, nice). The app never asks
students to describe weight or judge appearance negatively.
If a student writes something unkind about a real person,
request redo with gentle guidance.
