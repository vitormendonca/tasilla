==============================================================
TASILLA - A1 CONTENT / TOPIC 17: WEATHER & SEASONS
==============================================================
Unit 4 - Wider World | Difficulty dial: Topics 16-20
(10 new words, 4 options, inference questions, natural-pace
audio ~40s, reading texts 60-80 words, dictation 6-7 words,
60-second monologues, full recycling)

Grammar embedded: PRESENT CONTINUOUS (G10): It is raining. /
I am watching TV. + THE CONTRAST with simple present:
It rains every winter (habit - T7) vs It is raining NOW.
A1 includes continuous for NOW only - continuous for
future arrangements is A2 and stays out.
Can-do: Describe the weather and seasons
Recycles: T5 (cold - and hot finally goes active, as
promised in the T5 file), T7 (habits for the contrast),
T16 (do/does, hobbies per season), T8 (months), T12 (park)

TARGET LANGUAGE
Words (10): weather, sun, rain, snow, wind, hot, spring,
            summer, autumn, winter
Phrases:    What is the weather like? / It is hot. /
            It is raining now. / The sun is out. /
            my favorite season (season = intro-card word,
            like 'set' words in T2/T8)
Structures: am/is/are + verb-ing (I am writing, it is
            raining, we are swimming)
            -ing spelling flags: swim -> swimMing,
            dance -> dancing (e drops), rain -> raining
            CONTRAST: It rains every winter. <-> It is
            raining now.
NOTE: rainy/sunny/windy adjective forms NOT taught - A1
weather talk works with it is hot/cold + it is raining/
snowing + the sun is out. Degrees numbers appear as
passive exposure only.

==============================================================
LESSON 17.1 - VOCABULARY (auto-graded, 8 exercises, all tap)
==============================================================

Intro card:
  weather = sun? rain? hot? cold? - that!
  sun = the hot yellow one in the sky
  rain = water from the sky  |  snow = white, very cold!
  wind = air that moves - whooosh!
  hot = very very warm (summer!)
  seasons: spring (flowers) -> summer (hot!) ->
  autumn (wind) -> winter (cold, snow)
  + phrase bank: What is the weather like? | It is
    raining now. | The sun is out.
  + grammar note: NOW = am/is/are + -ING:
    It is raining. I am watching the rain!

EX 1 - Word meaning (MC)
Q: Water from the sky - but not white and cold. It is ___.
   a) rain   [CORRECT]
   b) snow
   c) wind
   d) sun
Feedback: Rain = water. Snow = white and very cold.

EX 2 - Word meaning (MC)
Q: The very hot season is ___.
   a) summer   [CORRECT]
   b) winter
   c) autumn
   d) spring
Feedback: Summer = hot! Winter = cold!

EX 3 - Gap-fill (tap the missing word)
Q: Whooosh! Close the window - the ___ is very strong
today!
   a) wind   [CORRECT]
   b) sun
   c) snow
   d) weather
Feedback: Wind = air that moves. Whooosh!

EX 4 - Gap-fill (tap the missing word)
Q: It is 35 degrees. It is very ___ - I need water!
   a) hot   [CORRECT]
   b) cold
   c) tired
   d) old
Feedback: Hot = the opposite of cold (T5!). Hot day ->
thirsty (T5!).

EX 5 - Odd one out (MC)
Q: Which one is NOT a season?
   a) winter
   b) weekend   [CORRECT]
   c) summer
   d) spring
Feedback: Weekend (T16) = Saturday + Sunday, in EVERY
season!

EX 6 - Dialogue completion (MC)
Q: A: What is the weather like in your city today?  B: ___
   a) It is cold, and it is raining.   [CORRECT]
   b) I am a doctor.
   c) It is next to the bank.
   d) Yes, I do.
Feedback: What is the weather like? -> hot/cold +
rain/sun/snow.

EX 7 - GRAMMAR: present continuous form (MC)
Q: Look! It ___ snowing! We ___ watching from the window!
   a) is / are   [CORRECT]
   b) are / is
   c) is / is
   d) does / do
Feedback: It IS snowing. We ARE watching. (am/is/are +
-ing = NOW)

EX 8 - GRAMMAR: the contrast (MC)
Q: It ___ every winter here. Look - it ___ right now!
   a) rains / is raining   [CORRECT]
   b) is raining / rains
   c) rain / raining
   d) rains / rains
Feedback: Every winter = habit -> rainS (T7). Right now
-> IS rainING. This contrast = strong A1!

Pass: 70% (6 of 8). XP: 10.

LESSON 17.2 - LISTENING (auto-graded, 1 audio + 5 exercises)
==============================================================

AUDIO SCRIPT (to record - 2 speakers, natural pace, phone
call, file: audio/a1/t17_listening_weather_call.mp3, ~40 s):

  Lala: Hi, Ivo! Where are you?
  Ivo:  At my sister's house in Recife! It is very hot
        here - the sun is out, and we are swimming all
        day!
  Lala: Swimming?! Ivo, here in Curitiba it is COLD.
        It is raining now, and the wind... terrible!
  Ivo:  Ha! Winter in Curitiba, Lala. It rains every

        watching films!

Max plays: 3.

EX 1 - Gist (MC)

   b) A trip by plane
   c) Ivo's sister's job
   d) Films at the cinema

EX 2 - Detail (MC)
Q: What is the weather like in Recife?
   a) Very hot, sun out   [CORRECT]
   b) Cold and raining
   c) Snowing
   d) The audio does not say

EX 3 - Detail: continuous (MC)
Q: What is Ivo doing in Recife?
   a) Swimming   [CORRECT]
   b) Watching films
   c) Drinking tea
   d) Working

EX 4 - INFERENCE (MC)
Q: Why is Lala at home with tea and films?
   a) Because it is cold and raining in Curitiba
      [CORRECT]
   b) Because she doesn't like the sun
   c) Because she works at home
   d) Because Ivo is in Recife
Feedback: Rain + cold + wind -> home, tea, films. You
put it together!

EX 5 - DICTATION (type what you hear - 7 words)
Audio repeats: It is cold and it is raining.
Correct answer: It is cold and it is raining
(accept case-insensitive, ignore punctuation)

Pass: 70% (4 of 5). XP: 10.

   b) A postcard from Recife
   c) A bus timetable
   d) A school notice

EX 2 - Detail (MC)
Q: Where is it snowing right now?
   a) In the mountains near Gramado   [CORRECT]
   b) In Recife
   c) In Curitiba
   d) In all cities

EX 3 - INFERENCE (MC)
Q: Which city is good for swimming TODAY?
   a) Recife   [CORRECT]
   b) Curitiba
   c) Gramado
   d) No city today
Feedback: Hot + sun all day + 'perfect for water' ->
Recife. The word 'swimming' is not there - the idea is!

  Every morning we drink hot coffee, and at night we

  every day?
  Kisses, Bibi
---------------------------------------

EX 4 - Gist (MC)
Q: What is this text?
   a) A postcard from Bibi's winter trip   [CORRECT]
   b) A weather app report
   c) Class notes
   d) A shopping list

EX 5 - Detail: continuous (MC)
Q: What is happening in Gramado at the moment Bibi

   a) It is snowing   [CORRECT]
   b) It is raining
   c) The sun is out
   d) It is hot

EX 6 - Grammar in context: the contrast (MC)
Q: 'Every morning we DRINK hot coffee' but 'it IS
SNOWING now'. Why the two forms?
   a) drink = every day (habit), is snowing = right now
      [CORRECT]
   b) They mean the same
   c) is snowing = every day, drink = now
   d) One of them is a mistake
Feedback: Habit -> simple (T7). Now -> am/is/are + -ing.
That is the whole G10 story!

LESSON 17.4 - SPEAKING (teacher-graded, 1 recorded task)
==============================================================

PROMPT (no example - free stage; 60-second monologue):
  Record a 60-second monologue: WEATHER AND ME.
  Cover:
  1. The weather in your city RIGHT NOW (It is... /
     It is raining / The sun is out) - look out the
     window!
  2. What you are doing now (I am recording... I am
     looking at...)
  3. Your favorite season + why (one reason is enough:
     In summer I swim...)
  4. One season habit vs now: In winter I... but today
     it is...

Max recording: 75 seconds (target: fill 60).
Tip: Point 1 and 2 = -ING (now). Points 3 and 4 = simple
(habits). Feel the difference as you speak!

RUBRIC:
  1. Fills ~60s, organized                  [ pass / not yet ]
  2. Present continuous correct (2+ uses)   [ pass / not yet ]
  3. Habit vs now contrast attempted        [ pass / not yet ]
  4. Understandable pronunciation           [ pass / not yet ]

XP: 15 on submit, +10 on approval.

LESSON 17.5 - WRITING (teacher-graded, MODE B - HANDWRITTEN,
FREE-WRITE)
==============================================================

SEVENTH PAPER TASK. Student writes BY HAND on paper for
THREE DIFFERENT DAYS, then uploads one photo of the full
diary.

TASK: Keep a short weather diary for 3 days.

Instructions shown in app:
  1. Take one piece of paper. Write: MY WEATHER DIARY.
  2. On 3 different days this week, write 2-3 lines:
     - the day (Monday...) - T8!
     - the weather (It is hot / cold / raining...)
     - one thing happening or one habit (I am drinking
       tea. / I swim every hot day.)
  3. After day 3, photograph the page and upload it.

Minimum requirements:
  - 3 dated entries (day names, spelled correctly)
  - 3 different weather descriptions
  - at least one present continuous sentence (...ing!)

RUBRIC:
  1. 3 entries, days correct                [ pass / not yet ]
  2. Weather words correct                  [ pass / not yet ]
  3. One correct -ing sentence              [ pass / not yet ]
  4. Handwriting readable (A1 bar)          [ pass / not yet ]
