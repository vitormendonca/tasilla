==============================================================
TASILLA - A1 CONTENT / TOPIC 7: DAILY ROUTINE & HABITS
==============================================================
Unit 2 - Everyday Life | Difficulty dial: Topics 6-10
(8 new words, 3 options, audio 4-5 sentences ~30s,
reading texts 40-50 words, T/F in reading, dictation 5-6
words, recycling systematic, scaffold fading)

Grammar embedded: SIMPLE PRESENT AFFIRMATIVE (G7) including
3rd person -s (works, eats, goes, watches, studies) + first
time expressions (G9 intro): every day, in the morning,
at night
Can-do: Say what you do every day
Recycles: T3 (family as sentence subjects), T5 (tired),
T6 (rooms, in + place), T2 (city, work in the city)

TARGET LANGUAGE
Words (8): get up, eat, drink, go, work, study, watch, sleep
Phrases:   I get up in the morning. / I go to work. /
           She works in the city. / He watches TV at night. /
           I study English every day.
Structures: simple present affirmative, all persons
            3rd person -s: works, eats, sleeps
            spelling flags: go -> goes, watch -> watches,
            study -> studies
            every day / in the morning / at night (chunks)
NOTE: clock times (at seven o'clock) NOT used - telling time
is Topic 8. Time here only via chunks in the morning /
at night / every day.
NOTE: do/does questions and negatives NOT here - that is G8
(Topic 16). All questions in this topic use to be
(Are you tired?) or statements.

==============================================================
LESSON 7.1 - VOCABULARY (auto-graded, 8 exercises, all tap)
==============================================================

Intro card:
  get up = out of bed!  |  eat = food goes in
  drink = water goes in  |  go = move to a place (go to work)
  work = your job  |  study = learn (study English!)
  watch = watch TV  |  sleep = in your bed at night
  + phrase bank: every day | in the morning | at night
  + grammar note: he/she + -s: works, eats, sleeps.
    Special: go -> goES, watch -> watchES, study -> studIES

EX 1 - Word meaning (MC)
Q: In the morning, you are in bed. Then you ___.
   a) get up   [CORRECT]
   b) sleep
   c) watch
Feedback: Get up = out of bed, start the day!

EX 2 - Word meaning (MC)
Q: You are thirsty (T5!). You ___ water.
   a) drink   [CORRECT]
   b) eat
   c) study
Feedback: Drink water. Eat food.

EX 3 - Gap-fill (tap the missing word)
Q: I ___ English every day. My teacher is happy!
   a) study   [CORRECT]
   b) sleep
   c) drink
Feedback: Study = learn. You study English with TASILLA!

EX 4 - Gap-fill (tap the missing word)
Q: At night, my family and I ___ TV in the bedroom.
   a) watch   [CORRECT]
   b) go
   c) get up
Feedback: Watch TV = look at TV.

EX 5 - Odd one out (MC)
Q: Which word is NOT an action (a verb)?
   a) eat
   b) kitchen   [CORRECT]
   c) work
Feedback: Kitchen is a room (Topic 6) - not an action.

EX 6 - Dialogue completion (MC)
Q: A: Why are you tired?  B: ___
   a) I work in the morning and I study at night.  [CORRECT]
   b) There are two bedrooms.
   c) I am from Curitiba.
Feedback: Tired? Because you work AND study - a big day!

EX 7 - GRAMMAR: 3rd person -s (MC)
Q: I work in the city. My sister ___ in the city too.
   a) works   [CORRECT]
   b) work
   c) is work
Feedback: he/she -> verb + S. I work / she workS.

EX 8 - GRAMMAR: -es spelling (MC)
Q: My father ___ to work every day. At night he ___ TV.
   a) goes / watches   [CORRECT]
   b) gos / watchs
   c) go / watch
Feedback: go -> goES. watch -> watchES.

Pass: 70% (6 of 8). XP: 10.

LESSON 7.4 - SPEAKING (teacher-graded, 1 recorded task)
==============================================================

PROMPT:
  Record yourself. Talk about your day.
  Say 4 things:
  1. Morning: I get up... I eat / I drink...
  2. Day: I go to... I work / I study...
  3. Night: I watch... / I sleep...
  4. ONE sentence about a family member with -s:
     My mother works... (T3 + new grammar!)
  Example: I get up in the morning. I drink water and I go
  to work. At night I study English. My brother watches TV
  every day!

Max recording: 60 seconds.
Tip: Morning -> day -> night. Follow your real day in order.

RUBRIC:
  1. 3+ routine verbs used correctly        [ pass / not yet ]
  2. Time chunks (morning/night/every day)  [ pass / not yet ]
  3. One 3rd person -s sentence             [ pass / not yet ]
  4. Understandable pronunciation           [ pass / not yet ]

XP: 15 on submit, +10 on approval.
