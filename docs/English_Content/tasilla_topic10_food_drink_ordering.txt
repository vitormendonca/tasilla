==============================================================
TASILLA - A1 CONTENT / TOPIC 10: FOOD & DRINK / ORDERING
==============================================================
Unit 2 - Everyday Life | Difficulty dial: Topics 6-10
(8 new words, 3 options, audio 4-5 sentences ~30s,
reading texts 40-50 words, T/F in reading, dictation 5-6
words, systematic recycling, scaffold fading)

Grammar embedded: A / AN full rule (G3: an + a-e-i-o-u sound:
an apple) + CAN I HAVE...? polite requests (G11 intro) +
I LIKE + noun (affirmative only - G7 pattern; do/does
questions and negatives stay in Topic 16)
Can-do: Order food and drink in a cafe; say what you like
Recycles: T7 (eat, drink + likes with -s), T9 (prices, How
much, pay), T5 (hungry, thirsty), T6 (there is/are, kitchen,
on the table)

TARGET LANGUAGE
Words (8): coffee, tea, milk, bread, rice, apple, juice, menu
Phrases:   Can I have a coffee, please? / Here you are. /
           I like rice. / She likes tea. / an apple /
           Is there tea? Yes, there is.
Structures: a + consonant sound (a coffee) / an + vowel
            sound (an apple)
            Can I have... please? (polite request chunk)
            like/likes + noun (affirmative only)
NOTE: NO some/any, NO much/many, NO countable/uncountable
work - all A2 (excluded list). Quantities here are a/an,
numbers, or the + noun only.
NOTE: don't like / Do you like...? NOT here - negatives and
questions with do/does are Topic 16 (G8).

LESSON 10.1 - VOCABULARY (auto-graded, 8 exercises, all tap)
==============================================================

Intro card:
  coffee = a hot, black drink  |  tea = a hot drink with
  leaves  |  milk = a white drink  |  bread = you eat it
  in the morning  |  rice = small and white, with lunch
  apple = a red or green fruit  |  juice = a fruit drink
  menu = the list of food and prices in a cafe
  + phrase bank: Can I have..., please? | Here you are. |
    I like...
  + grammar note: a coffee, a tea BUT an apple (a-e-i-o-u
    sound -> an)

EX 1 - Word meaning (MC)
Q: A white drink from a cow? It is ___.
   a) milk   [CORRECT]
   b) coffee
   c) rice
Feedback: Milk = white drink. Coffee = black drink.

EX 2 - Word meaning (MC)
Q: In a cafe, the list of food and prices is the ___.
   a) menu   [CORRECT]
   b) bread
   c) money
Feedback: Menu = the list. Money (T9) pays for it!

EX 3 - Gap-fill (tap the missing word)
Q: I am thirsty. Apple ___, please!
   a) juice   [CORRECT]
   b) bread
   c) rice
Feedback: Thirsty (T5) -> a drink -> juice!

EX 4 - Gap-fill (tap the missing word)
Q: In the morning I eat ___ and I drink coffee.
   a) bread   [CORRECT]
   b) tea
   c) juice
Feedback: You EAT bread. You DRINK tea and juice (T7 verbs!).

EX 5 - Odd one out (MC)
Q: Which one is NOT a drink?
   a) tea
   b) rice   [CORRECT]
   c) juice
Feedback: You eat rice. You drink tea and juice.

EX 6 - Dialogue completion (MC)
Q: Waiter: Good morning! ...  You: ___
   a) Can I have a coffee, please?   [CORRECT]
   b) There are two bedrooms.
   c) My birthday is in May.
Feedback: In a cafe, order with: Can I have..., please?

EX 7 - GRAMMAR: a / an (MC)
Q: Can I have ___ apple and ___ tea, please?
   a) an / a   [CORRECT]
   b) a / an
   c) an / an
Feedback: AN apple (a-e-i-o-u sound). A tea.

EX 8 - GRAMMAR: like / likes (MC)
Q: I ___ coffee. My mother ___ tea.
   a) like / likes   [CORRECT]
   b) likes / like
   c) like / like
Feedback: I like. He/she likeS (T7 rule - same -s!).

Pass: 70% (6 of 8). XP: 10.

   a) Three dollars   [CORRECT]
   b) Two dollars
   c) One dollar

EX 3 - True / False (MC)
Q: Cafe Sol is open on Sunday. True or false?
   a) True
   b) False   [CORRECT]  (Monday to SATURDAY)

TEXT B (47 words) - Chat messages format:
---------------------------------------
  Odo: I am hungry, Ema!
  Ema: Me too! There is rice and bread in the kitchen.
  Odo: I like rice! And juice?
  Ema: There is apple juice on the table.
  Odo: My sister likes tea. Is there tea?
  Ema: Yes, there is. It is very good tea!
---------------------------------------

EX 4 - Gist (MC)
Q: What are Odo and Ema talking about?
   a) Food and drink at home   [CORRECT]
   b) A cafe menu
   c) Prices in a shop

EX 5 - Detail (MC)
Q: What does Odo like?
   a) Rice   [CORRECT]
   b) Tea
   c) Milk

EX 6 - Detail + 3rd person (MC)
Q: Who likes tea?
   a) Odo's sister   [CORRECT]
   b) Odo
   c) Ema
Feedback: Odo says: my sister LIKES tea (-s = she!).

Pass: 70% (5 of 6). XP: 10.

LESSON 10.4 - SPEAKING (teacher-graded, 1 recorded task)
==============================================================

PROMPT (no example - free stage):
  Record yourself. You are in a cafe - order!
  Say 4 things:
  1. Greet the waiter (Good morning!)
  2. Order 2 things: Can I have ... and ..., please?
     (careful: a coffee, but AN apple!)
  3. Ask the price: How much is it? (T9)
  4. Say one thing you like: I like...

Max recording: 60 seconds.
Tip: Order things YOU really like - it is easy to
remember real words.

RUBRIC:
  1. Polite order with Can I have + please  [ pass / not yet ]
  2. a/an correct                           [ pass / not yet ]
  3. How much question (T9 recycling)       [ pass / not yet ]
  4. Understandable pronunciation           [ pass / not yet ]
