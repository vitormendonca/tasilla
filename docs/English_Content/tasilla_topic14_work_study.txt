==============================================================
TASILLA - A1 CONTENT / TOPIC 14: WORK & STUDY
==============================================================
Unit 3 - Out in the World | Difficulty dial: Topics 11-15
(8 new words, 4 options, 'not stated' live, audio ~35s
dialogues, reading texts 50-60 words, dictation 6 words,
free-write)

Grammar embedded: CAN + verb = ABILITY (G11 second half:
I can speak English - can never takes -s) + A/AN with jobs
(I am A teacher) + 3rd person -s consolidation (G7)
Can-do: Talk about your job or studies
Recycles: T12 (hospital, office joins places), T7 (work,
study as routine + -s), T2 (email, city), T3 (possessive
's), T8 (times), T11 (shop)

TARGET LANGUAGE
Words (8): job, teacher, doctor, student, office, speak,
           write, help
(teacher and student promoted from sight words - used
since Topic 1 - to active, tested vocabulary)
Phrases:   What is your job? / I am a teacher. / She works
           in an office. / I can speak English. / He can
           help you. / I am a student at night!
Structures: a/an + job: a doctor, a teacher, an office
            can + base verb, ALL persons the same:
            I can speak / she can speak (never cans,
            never can speaks)
NOTE: can here = ability. Can as polite request was T10-11.
Both halves of G11 are now live. Job list stays tiny
(teacher, doctor, student + free slot for the student's
real job via teacher support) - A1 certificate needs
'talk about your job', not a professions catalogue.

LESSON 14.1 - VOCABULARY (auto-graded, 8 exercises, all tap)
==============================================================

Intro card:
  job = your work: teacher, doctor...
  teacher = teaches students (your English teacher!)
  doctor = works at the hospital
  student = studies at school
  office = the work room with tables, chairs, computers
  speak = talk (speak English!)  |  write = make words
  on paper  |  help = do good things for a person
  + phrase bank: What is your job? | I am a... |
    I can speak English!
  + grammar note: I am A teacher / AN office worker
    (a/an + job). can + verb: NO -s, ever!

EX 1 - Word meaning (MC)
Q: This person works at the hospital. ___
   a) A doctor   [CORRECT]
   b) A student
   c) A teacher
   d) A taxi
Feedback: Doctor -> hospital (T12!). Teacher -> school.

EX 2 - Word meaning (MC)
Q: The work room with tables, chairs and computers is
an ___.
   a) office   [CORRECT]
   b) airport
   c) bedroom
   d) bathroom
Feedback: Office = the work room.

EX 3 - Gap-fill (tap the missing word)
Q: My little brother is 6. I ___ him with his school
things every day.
   a) help   [CORRECT]
   b) write
   c) drink
   d) travel
Feedback: Help = do good things for a person.

EX 4 - Gap-fill (tap the missing word)
Q: In my English class, we ___ and we write.
   a) speak   [CORRECT]
   b) sleep
   c) pay
   d) turn
Feedback: Speak = talk. Speak English every day!

EX 5 - Odd one out (MC)
Q: Which one is NOT a job?
   a) teacher
   b) juice   [CORRECT]
   c) doctor
   d) student
Feedback: Juice is a drink (Topic 10)!

EX 6 - Dialogue completion (MC)
Q: A: What is your job?  B: ___
   a) I am a teacher. I work at a school in Santos.
      [CORRECT]
   b) I am hungry.
   c) It is ten dollars.
   d) Turn left at the bank.
Feedback: What is your job? -> I am a/an + job.

EX 7 - GRAMMAR: a/an + job (MC)
Q: She is ___ doctor. He works in ___ office.
   a) a / an   [CORRECT]
   b) an / a
   c) a / a
   d) an / an
Feedback: A doctor. AN office (a-e-i-o-u sound - T10 rule!).

EX 8 - GRAMMAR: can (MC)
Q: I ___ speak English. My sister ___ speak English too.
   a) can / can   [CORRECT]
   b) can / cans
   c) cans / can
   d) can / can speaks
Feedback: CAN never changes: I can, she can, we can!

Pass: 70% (6 of 8). XP: 10.

   a) At her father's shop   [CORRECT]
   b) At the office

LESSON 14.4 - SPEAKING (teacher-graded, 1 recorded task)
==============================================================

PROMPT (no example - free stage):
  Record yourself. You, at work or at school.
  Say 4 things:
  1. Your job or study: I am a/an... (student? teacher?
     your real job - ask your teacher for your job word!)
  2. Where + when: I work/study in/at... in the morning /
     at night (T7-8)
  3. TWO things you can do: I can speak... I can write...
     I can help...
  4. One person in your family: his/her job with -s
     (My mother works...)

Max recording: 60 seconds.
Tip: Your real job word is YOUR word - learn it well!

RUBRIC:
  1. a/an + job correct                     [ pass / not yet ]
  2. can + verb correct (2 examples)        [ pass / not yet ]
  3. 3rd person -s for family member        [ pass / not yet ]
  4. Understandable pronunciation           [ pass / not yet ]

XP: 15 on submit, +10 on approval.

PRODUCTION NOTES
==============================================================
- 1 audio to record (~35s, 2 speakers) + 1 review line (~12s)
- languages, company, computers, hour = passive exposure
- can = ability joins can = request (T10-11): G11 complete.
  Flag for the teacher: students often write she cans -
  EX 8 feedback targets exactly this
- Job words kept minimal by design; the teacher supplies
  each student's real job word (dashboard note) - it then
  counts as personal vocabulary in speaking/writing rubrics
- No do-questions: What is your job? carries the topic
  (to be question). 'Where do you work?' does NOT appear
  until T16
- Recycling check: hospital/office extend T12 places;
  father's shop ('s, T3 + T11); email (T2); times (T8);
  pay + dollars (T9)
