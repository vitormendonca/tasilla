==============================================================
TASILLA - A1 FINAL TESTS / SPEAKING PATH FINAL TEST
(STEP 27 OF 27 OF THE SPEAKING SKILL PATH)
==============================================================
Covers: Topics 1-20. Teacher-graded. Passing (teacher
approval) completes the Speaking path and finalizes the
Speaking skill score for the certificate.

DESIGN:
- 3 recorded parts in ONE session (the app records
  part by part, then submits together)
- Mirrors a real A1 oral exam: interview -> description
  -> monologue
- Teacher grades a 6-criteria rubric across the 3 parts
- XP: 30 on submit, +20 on approval

==============================================================
PART 1 - INTERVIEW (target ~60 seconds)
==============================================================

The app plays 6 recorded questions, one at a time, with
a pause for the student's spoken answer after each
(question audio files:
audio/a1/finaltest_speaking_q1-q6.mp3, natural pace):

  Q1: Hello! What is your name, and how do you spell it?
  Q2: How old are you, and where are you from?
  Q3: Who is in your family? Tell me about one person.
  Q4: What do you do every day - in the morning and
      at night?
  Q5: What is the weather like today?
  Q6: Do you like sport? What do you do in your free
      time?

Student instruction: Answer each question in 1-2 full
sentences. Short answers ('yes') are not enough - show
your English!

==============================================================
PART 2 - DESCRIPTION TASK (target ~45 seconds)
==============================================================

The app shows this scene card (text, no image needed -
students imagine or use their real life):

  YOUR PERSON: Choose one real person (family or
  friend). Describe:
  - name + who they are (his/her!)
  - what they look like (is + adjective, has + hair/
    eyes) + one COLOUR
  - what they are wearing today, if you can see them -
    or what they wear every day
  - one thing they can do + one thing they don't do

==============================================================
PART 3 - MONOLOGUE (target 60+ seconds)
==============================================================

Choose ONE topic card (the app offers all three):
  A) MY DAY - morning to night, with times and
     transport
  B) MY HOME AND MY CITY - rooms, places, directions
     to your house
  C) MY LIFE NOW - job/study, hobbies, your animal or
     an animal you know

Speak for at least 60 seconds. No reading from notes!

Max total recording (3 parts): 4 minutes.

==============================================================
RUBRIC (6 criteria, across all 3 parts)
==============================================================
  1. Part 1: answers all 6 questions with
     full sentences                          [ pass / not yet ]
  2. Part 2: description systems correct
     (is/has/wearing + colour)               [ pass / not yet ]
  3. Part 3: fills 60s, organized, on topic  [ pass / not yet ]
  4. Grammar range: to be, present + -s,
     do-system, can, one -ing                [ pass / not yet ]
  5. Vocabulary range: words from 8+
     different topics across the 3 parts     [ pass / not yet ]
  6. Understandable throughout (A1 bar: a
     patient listener follows easily)        [ pass / not yet ]

Approval bar: 5 of 6, criterion 6 mandatory.
Teacher action: Approve / Request redo of ONE part
(other parts keep their recording).

==============================================================
PRODUCTION NOTES
==============================================================
- 6 short question audios to record (Q1-Q6, one voice,
  natural pace, friendly)
- Engine: multi-part recording session with per-part
  re-record BEFORE submit; after submit, teacher can
  request redo per part
- This test + 20 speaking lessons + 2 portfolios = the
  speaking evidence trail; the final test is the exam-
  like sample
- Rubric criterion 5 operationalizes 'full recycling'
  for the teacher: 8+ topics is a checkbox, not a count
  exercise - the dashboard shows the topic list as
  hints
