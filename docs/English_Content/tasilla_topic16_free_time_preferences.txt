==============================================================
TASILLA - A1 CONTENT / TOPIC 16: FREE TIME & PREFERENCES
==============================================================
Unit 4 - Wider World | Difficulty dial: Topics 16-20
(10 new words, 4 options, INFERENCE questions enter,
NATURAL-PACE audio ~40s, reading texts 60-80 words,
dictation 6-7 words, 60-second speaking monologues,
FULL recycling - all previous topics fair game)

Grammar embedded: SIMPLE PRESENT NEGATIVES & QUESTIONS
(G8 - the big unlock): Do you...? / Does she...? /
I don't... / He doesn't... + short answers (Yes, I do. /
No, she doesn't.). The T15 survival chunks (How do you
spell...? What does ... mean?) are now ANALYZED - students
finally see the machine inside phrases they already use.
Can-do: Talk about hobbies, likes and dislikes
Recycles: T7 (verbs + routine), T10 (like/likes), T8
(weekend joins days), T14 (can), T5 (happy)

TARGET LANGUAGE
Words (10): hobby, music, film, game, sport, play, dance,
            swim, sing, weekend
Phrases:    What do you do in your free time? / Do you
            like music? / Yes, I do. / No, I don't. /
            I don't dance. / She doesn't play games. /
            my favorite film (favorite = chunk in the
            phrase bank)
Structures: do/does questions + don't/doesn't negatives
            + short answers
            like + noun (I like music - like + -ing NOT
            taught; A1 bar is like + thing)
NOTE: dial shift is big here - flag it to students on the
lesson intro: audio is faster, questions can ask WHY
(inference), speaking is a 60-second solo. The grammar
payoff: questions and negatives make real conversation
possible for the first time.

==============================================================
LESSON 16.1 - VOCABULARY (auto-graded, 8 exercises, all tap)
==============================================================

Intro card:
  hobby = a thing you love to do in free time
  music = you listen to it (and dance!)
  film = you watch it (one hour, two hours...)
  game = you play it (phone, cards, football...)
  sport = swim, run, football...
  play = games, sport, music!  |  dance = move with music
  swim = move in the water  |  sing = music with your voice
  weekend = Saturday + Sunday
  + phrase bank: Do you like...? | Yes, I do / No, I
    don't | my favorite...
  + grammar note: questions with DO/DOES, negatives with
    DON'T/DOESN'T - and the -s moves to DOES:
    She playS -> DOES she play? / She DOESN'T play.

EX 1 - Word meaning (MC)
Q: Saturday and Sunday together are the ___.
   a) weekend   [CORRECT]
   b) hobby
   c) week
   d) month
Feedback: Weekend = the END of the week: Saturday + Sunday.

EX 2 - Word meaning (MC)
Q: You move in the water. You ___.
   a) swim   [CORRECT]
   b) dance
   c) sing
   d) play
Feedback: Swim = water! Dance = music + moves.

EX 3 - Gap-fill (tap the missing word)
Q: My ___ is music - I listen every day and I sing!
   a) hobby   [CORRECT]
   b) game
   c) job
   d) office
Feedback: Hobby = your free-time love. Job (T14) = work!

EX 4 - Gap-fill (tap the missing word)
Q: We watch a ___ every Saturday night.
   a) film   [CORRECT]
   b) sport
   c) music
   d) bread
Feedback: You WATCH a film (watch - T7!).

EX 5 - Odd one out (MC)
Q: Which one is NOT a free-time word?
   a) game
   b) ticket   [CORRECT]
   c) music
   d) sport
Feedback: Ticket is for travel (T13) - unless it is a
film ticket!

EX 6 - Dialogue completion (MC)
Q: A: Do you like sport?  B: ___
   a) Yes, I do! I swim every weekend.   [CORRECT]
   b) Yes, I am.
   c) It is next to the park.
   d) I am a teacher.
Feedback: Do you...? -> Yes, I DO / No, I DON'T
(not Yes, I am - that is for ARE you...? T5!).

EX 7 - GRAMMAR: don't / doesn't (MC)
Q: I ___ dance. My father ___ swim.
   a) don't / doesn't   [CORRECT]
   b) doesn't / don't
   c) don't / don't
   d) not / not
Feedback: I DON'T. He/she DOESN'T. (The -s moves into
DOES!)

EX 8 - GRAMMAR: do / does questions (MC)
Q: ___ you play games? ___ your sister play too?
   a) Do / Does   [CORRECT]
   b) Does / Do
   c) Do / Do
   d) Are / Is
Feedback: DO you...? DOES she...? And the main verb stays
clean: Does she PLAY (never plays).

Pass: 70% (6 of 8). XP: 10.

LESSON 16.2 - LISTENING (auto-graded, 1 audio + 5 exercises)
==============================================================

AUDIO SCRIPT (to record - 2 speakers, NATURAL PACE from
this topic - normal rhythm, still clear,
file: audio/a1/t16_listening_do_you_like_music.mp3, ~40 s):

  Rico: Bel, do you like music?
  Bel:  Yes, I do! I dance every weekend - it is my
        hobby. And you?
  Rico: I don't dance. But I play games, and I swim on
        Saturdays.
  Bel:  Nice! Does your brother swim with you?
  Rico: No, he doesn't. He watches films every night -
        two films, three films...
  Bel:  Every night?! OK - films are HIS hobby!

Max plays: 3.

EX 1 - Gist (MC)
Q: What do Rico and Bel talk about?
   a) Their hobbies and free time   [CORRECT]
   b) Their jobs
   c) A film at the cinema
   d) The weekend weather

EX 2 - Detail (MC)
Q: What is Bel's hobby?
   a) Dancing - every weekend   [CORRECT]
   b) Swimming on Saturdays
   c) Playing games
   d) Watching films

EX 3 - Detail: short answer (MC)
Q: Does Rico's brother swim?
   a) No, he doesn't   [CORRECT]
   b) Yes, he does
   c) Only on Saturdays
   d) The audio does not say

EX 4 - INFERENCE enters (MC)
Q: Why does Bel say films are the brother's hobby?
   a) Because he watches films every night   [CORRECT]
   b) Because he swims every day
   c) Because he doesn't like films
   d) Because he works at a cinema
Feedback: Every night, two or three films -> that IS a
hobby! The audio does not say the word directly - YOU
put it together. New question type!

EX 5 - DICTATION (type what you hear - 6 words)
Audio repeats: I play games and I swim.
Correct answer: I play games and I swim
(accept case-insensitive, ignore punctuation)

Pass: 70% (4 of 5). XP: 10.

LESSON 16.3 - READING (auto-graded, 2 texts, 3 questions each)
==============================================================

TEXT A (66 words) - Profile page format:
---------------------------------------
  CLUB PAGE - MEET OUR MEMBERS!
  Name: Tina, 24, from Santos.
  Hobbies: I swim every morning before work, and I play
  the guitar at night. I don't watch TV - I don't like
  it! On the weekend I sing with my friends at the park.
  Favorite music: everything! I dance to all of it.
  Do you like music too? Come to the club on Saturday!
---------------------------------------

EX 1 - Gist (MC)
Q: What is this text?
   a) A club member's profile   [CORRECT]
   b) A job advertisement
   c) A cafe menu
   d) Class notes

EX 2 - Detail (MC)
Q: What does Tina do every morning?
   a) She swims   [CORRECT]
   b) She sings at the park
   c) She watches TV
   d) She plays the guitar

EX 3 - INFERENCE (MC)
Q: Which sentence about Tina is TRUE?
   a) Music is a very big part of her life   [CORRECT]
   b) She likes TV very much
   c) She doesn't like the weekend
   d) She works at the park
Feedback: Guitar + singing + dancing to ALL music ->
music is her life. The text doesn't say it - it SHOWS it.

TEXT B (62 words) - Chat messages format:
---------------------------------------
  Nino: Gala! Do you play games at night?
  Gala: No, I don't. At night I read - one book every
        week!
  Nino: One book a WEEK?! I read one book a year...
  Gala: Ha! But you swim and you dance, Nino. I don't
        do sport.
  Nino: True! So: you read, I swim. On Saturday - film
        at my house? Do you like old films?
  Gala: Yes, I do!!
---------------------------------------

EX 4 - Gist (MC)
Q: What do Nino and Gala talk about?
   a) Their different hobbies + a film on Saturday
      [CORRECT]
   b) Homework for English class
   c) A new book shop
   d) A trip by train

EX 5 - Detail: negative (MC)
Q: What does Gala NOT do?
   a) Sport   [CORRECT]
   b) Read books
   c) Watch films
   d) Talk to Nino

EX 6 - INFERENCE (MC)
Q: Gala reads one book every week. Nino reads one book
a year. What is true?
   a) Gala reads much more than Nino   [CORRECT]
   b) Nino reads more books
   c) They read the same books
   d) Gala doesn't like books
Feedback: One a week vs one a year - count it! (And no
comparative grammar needed: 'much more' is given in the
option, you only pick the true one.)

Pass: 70% (5 of 6). XP: 10.

LESSON 16.4 - SPEAKING (teacher-graded, 1 recorded task)
==============================================================

PROMPT (no example - free stage; FIRST 60-SECOND MONOLOGUE):
  Record a 60-second monologue: MY FREE TIME.
  Speak alone, no dialogue - like a little radio show!
  Cover:
  1. Your hobby or hobbies + when (weekend, at night...)
  2. One thing you DON'T do or DON'T like (I don't...)
  3. One person in your family: what he/she does or
     doesn't do (She plays... / He doesn't dance...)
  4. Finish with a question to ME: Do you like...?

Max recording: 75 seconds (target: fill 60).
Tip: 60 seconds is long! Plan 4 small parts, breathe,
speak - don't stop at 20 seconds.

RUBRIC:
  1. Fills ~60s on topic, organized         [ pass / not yet ]
  2. don't/doesn't used correctly           [ pass / not yet ]
  3. Do/Does question correct               [ pass / not yet ]
  4. Understandable pronunciation           [ pass / not yet ]

XP: 15 on submit, +10 on approval.

PRODUCTION NOTES
==============================================================
- 1 audio to record (~40s, 2 speakers, NATURAL PACE - this
  is the pace shift topic; keep articulation clean but
  drop the slow-motion pauses of T1-15) + 1 review line

  (guitar/football never tested as target vocabulary)

  combine two facts, or name the obvious pattern) - never

  but comparative GRAMMAR is never produced or tested -
  the A2 fence holds
