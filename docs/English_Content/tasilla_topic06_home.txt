==============================================================
TASILLA - A1 CONTENT / TOPIC 6: HOME & WHERE YOU LIVE
==============================================================
Unit 2 - Everyday Life | Difficulty dial: Topics 6-10
(8 new words, 3 options, audio 4-5 sentences ~30s,
reading texts 40-50 words, T/F enters reading, dictation
5-6 words, systematic recycling starts, scaffold fading)

Grammar embedded: THERE IS / THERE ARE (G5) + prepositions
IN / ON / UNDER (G6) + A/AN with singular nouns (G3 intro)
Can-do: Name rooms and furniture; describe your home simply
Recycles: T2 (city, address), T3 (family, our), T4 (big,
small, very), T5 (happy, cold)

TARGET LANGUAGE
Words (8): house, room, bedroom, kitchen, bathroom, bed,
           table, chair
Phrases:   There is a table in the kitchen. / There are two
           bedrooms. / Where is the cat?... -> Where is my
           phone? / It is on the table. / under the bed /
           My house is small but happy!
Structures: there is + a + singular / there are + number +
            plural (-s)
            in / on / under + the + noun
            a/an: a bedroom, a chair (an enters fully in T10
            with an apple - here only noted on intro card)
NOTE: apartment appears as passive exposure in Text B only.
live (verb) NOT used - verbs start in T7 (simple present);
home descriptions here use to be + there is/are only.

==============================================================
LESSON 6.1 - VOCABULARY (auto-graded, 8 exercises, all tap)
==============================================================

Intro card:
  house = your building  |  room = one space in a house
  bedroom = room with a bed (sleep!)
  kitchen = room for food  |  bathroom = room for a shower
  bed = you sleep in it  |  table = you eat on it
  chair = you sit on it
  + phrase bank: There is a... | There are two... |
    in / on / under
  + grammar note: a + word (a bed); an + a-e-i-o-u word
    (an address)

EX 1 - Word meaning (MC)
Q: You sleep in this room. It is the ___.
   a) bedroom   [CORRECT]
   b) kitchen
   c) bathroom
Feedback: Bedroom = bed + room. You sleep there.

EX 2 - Word meaning (MC)
Q: Your food is in this room. It is the ___.
   a) kitchen   [CORRECT]
   b) bedroom
   c) chair
Feedback: Kitchen = the room for food.

EX 3 - Gap-fill (tap the missing word)
Q: I am tired. Where is my ___? I need sleep!
   a) bed   [CORRECT]
   b) table
   c) kitchen
Feedback: Tired (T5!) -> you need your BED.

EX 4 - Gap-fill (tap the missing word)
Q: Dinner time! The food is on the ___.
   a) table   [CORRECT]
   b) chair
   c) bathroom
Feedback: Food goes ON the table. You sit ON a chair.

EX 5 - Odd one out (MC)
Q: Which word is NOT a room?
   a) bathroom
   b) chair   [CORRECT]
   c) bedroom
Feedback: A chair is furniture - it is IN a room.

EX 6 - Dialogue completion (MC)
Q: A: Is your house big?  B: ___
   a) No, it is small. But it is very happy!   [CORRECT]
   b) Yes, I am 21 years old.
   c) It is on the table.
Feedback: Is your HOUSE big? -> answer about the house (it).

EX 7 - GRAMMAR: there is / there are (MC)
Q: ___ two bedrooms in my house. ___ a table in the kitchen.
   a) There are / There is   [CORRECT]
   b) There is / There are
   c) There are / There are
Feedback: There ARE + two (plural). There IS + a (one).

EX 8 - GRAMMAR: in / on / under (MC)
Q: My phone is not ON the table. It is ___ the table!
   a) under   [CORRECT]
   b) in
   c) is
Feedback: On = on top. Under = below. In = inside.

Pass: 70% (6 of 8). XP: 10.

LESSON 6.4 - SPEAKING (teacher-graded, 1 recorded task)
==============================================================

PROMPT:
  Record yourself. Describe your home.
  Say 4 things:
  1. House big or small? (My house / apartment is...)
  2. The rooms (There are ... bedrooms / There is a...)
  3. ONE thing and where it is (There is a table in the...)
  4. One family sentence (T3): My mother is in the kitchen...
  Example: My house is small. There are two bedrooms.
  There is a big table in the kitchen. My brother is in
  his bedroom!

Max recording: 60 seconds.
Tip: Walk around your home (in your head!) room by room.

RUBRIC:
  1. Describes home with to be + adjective  [ pass / not yet ]
  2. There is / there are used correctly    [ pass / not yet ]
  3. One in/on/under phrase                 [ pass / not yet ]
  4. Understandable pronunciation           [ pass / not yet ]

XP: 15 on submit, +10 on approval.

LESSON 6.5 - WRITING (teacher-graded, Mode A typed, SCAFFOLDED
- FADING: shorter model, 3 gaps)
==============================================================

STEP 1 - MODEL (student reads):
  My apartment is small but very nice.
  There are two bedrooms.
  There is a big table in the kitchen.
  My cat sleeps under my bed!

STEP 2 - GAP-FILL the model (tap: are / in / under) - 3 gaps
now, not 4 (scaffold fading):
  There ___ two bedrooms.            -> are
  There is a big table ___ the kitchen.  -> in
  My cat sleeps ___ my bed!          -> under
  (warm-up, auto-checked, not scored)

STEP 3 - WRITE YOUR OWN VERSION (typed, teacher-graded):
Prompt: Describe YOUR home (house, apartment, or room).
Minimum requirements:
  - 4 to 5 sentences
  - there is AND there are, each at least once
  - one in/on/under phrase
  - one adjective (big / small / nice - T4)

RUBRIC:
  1. 4-5 sentences about a real home        [ pass / not yet ]
  2. There is/are match singular/plural     [ pass / not yet ]
  3. Preposition used correctly             [ pass / not yet ]
  4. Readable spelling (A1 bar)             [ pass / not yet ]

XP: 15 on submit, +10 on approval.

SENSITIVITY NOTE for teachers: homes vary widely. 'Your home'
can be one room, a shared house, a grandparent's home. Never
question the home a student describes; rubric accepts any
real living space.
