==============================================================
TASILLA - A1 CONTENT / TOPIC 3: FAMILY & RELATIONSHIPS
==============================================================
Unit 1 - Foundation | Difficulty dial: Topics 1-5
(6 new words, 3 options, slow audio 3-4 sentences,
reading texts 30-40 words, dictation 4-5 words)

Grammar embedded: possessive 'S (Ana's brother) + possessive
adjectives HIS / HER / OUR (extends T2's my/your)
Can-do: Talk about family members
Recycles: T1 (My name is, I am), T2 (age, city, Are you...?)

TARGET LANGUAGE
Words (6): mother, father, brother, sister, family, friend
Phrases:   This is my mother. / I have a brother. /
           His name is... / Her name is... / How old is she?
Structures: Ana's brother / my father's car (possessive 's)
            his / her / our + noun
            She is... / He is... (3rd person to be, natural
            extension of T1-2 to be work)

==============================================================
LESSON 3.1 - VOCABULARY (auto-graded, 8 exercises, all tap)
==============================================================

Intro card:
  mother = your mom
  father = your dad
  brother = a boy/man with the same parents
  sister = a girl/woman with the same parents
  family = mother + father + brothers + sisters...
  friend = a person you like, not family
  + phrase bank: This is my... | I have a... | His name is... |
    Her name is...

EX 1 - Word meaning (MC)
Q: Your mother and your father are your ___.
   a) parents / family   [CORRECT]
   b) friends
   c) sisters
Feedback: Mother + father = family (parents).

EX 2 - Word meaning (MC)
Q: A girl with the same mother and father as you is your ___.
   a) sister   [CORRECT]
   b) brother
   c) friend
Feedback: Sister = girl. Brother = boy.

EX 3 - Gap-fill (tap the missing word)
Q: This is Paulo. He is not my family. He is my ___.
   a) friend   [CORRECT]
   b) father
   c) sister
Feedback: A friend is a person you like who is not family.

EX 4 - Gap-fill (tap the missing word)
Q: I have one brother and one ___. Her name is Lia.
   a) sister   [CORRECT]
   b) father
   c) friend
Feedback: HER name = a girl -> sister.

EX 5 - Odd one out (MC)
Q: Which one is NOT a family word?
   a) mother
   b) city   [CORRECT]
   c) brother
Feedback: City is from Topic 2 - it is a place, not family.

EX 6 - Dialogue completion (MC)
Q: A: Who is this?  B: ___
   a) This is my mother.   [CORRECT]
   b) I am from Brazil.
   c) Good morning.
Feedback: Who is this? -> answer with This is my...

EX 7 - GRAMMAR: possessive 's (MC)
Q: Ana has a brother. He is ___ brother.
   a) Ana's   [CORRECT]
   b) Ana is
   c) Anas
Feedback: Ana's brother = the brother of Ana. Use apostrophe + s.

EX 8 - GRAMMAR: his / her (MC)
Q: This is my father. ___ name is Carlos.
   a) His   [CORRECT]
   b) Her
   c) Your
Feedback: Father = man -> HIS. Mother = woman -> HER.

Pass: 70% (6 of 8). XP: 10.

==============================================================
LESSON 3.4 - SPEAKING (teacher-graded, 1 recorded task)
==============================================================

PROMPT:
  Record yourself. Talk about 2 people in your family.
  For each person say:
  1. Who they are (This is my... / I have a...)
  2. Their name (His name is... / Her name is...)
  3. One more thing (age or city - remember Topic 2!)
  Example: I have a sister. Her name is Julia. She is 15
  years old. This is my father. His name is Marcos. He is
  from Santos.

Max recording: 45 seconds.
Tip: Choose the 2 people BEFORE you record.

RUBRIC:
  1. Presents 2 family members             [ pass / not yet ]
  2. Uses his/her correctly at least once  [ pass / not yet ]
  3. Adds age or city (T2 recycling)       [ pass / not yet ]
  4. Understandable pronunciation          [ pass / not yet ]

XP: 15 on submit, +10 on approval.

==============================================================
LESSON 3.5 - WRITING (teacher-graded, Mode A typed, SCAFFOLDED)
==============================================================

STEP 1 - MODEL (student reads):
  My family is small.
  My mother's name is Sonia. She is 48 years old.
  I have one brother. His name is Beto.
  He is my friend too!

STEP 2 - GAP-FILL the model (tap: name / His / have / mother's):
  My ___ name is Sonia.        -> mother's
  She is 48 years old. I ___ one brother.  -> have
  ___ name is Beto.            -> His
  (warm-up, auto-checked, not scored)

STEP 3 - WRITE YOUR OWN VERSION (typed, teacher-graded):
Prompt: Write about YOUR family (or people close to you).
Minimum requirements:
  - 4 sentences
  - Use: one possessive 's (my mother's name...),
    his or her at least once, one age or city

RUBRIC:
  1. 4 sentences about real people          [ pass / not yet ]
  2. Possessive 's used correctly           [ pass / not yet ]
  3. His/her matches the person             [ pass / not yet ]
  4. Readable spelling (A1 bar)             [ pass / not yet ]

XP: 15 on submit, +10 on approval.

SENSITIVITY NOTE for teachers: family structures vary. The
prompt allows 'people close to you' - never force students to
describe a standard family. Rubric criterion 1 accepts any 2
real people (friend, grandmother, guardian...).
