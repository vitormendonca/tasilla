SECTION A - LISTENING (3 items, 1 new audio ~25s)
==============================================================

AUDIO SCRIPT (to record - 2 speakers, slow,
file: audio/a1/mixrevD_listening.mp3):

  Customer: Good morning! Can I have two coffees and an
            apple, please?
  Waiter:   Yes! The coffees are four dollars, and the
            apple is one dollar. Five dollars, please.
  Customer: Here you are!
  Waiter:   Thank you!

EX 1 - Gist: What is this conversation?
   a) A person orders in a cafe   [CORRECT]
   b) A person buys a bed
   c) Two friends talk about the week

EX 2 - What does the customer order?
   a) Two coffees and an apple   [CORRECT]
   b) Two teas and bread
   c) An apple juice

EX 3 - How much is it all?
   a) Five dollars   [CORRECT]
   b) Fifteen dollars
   c) Fifty dollars
