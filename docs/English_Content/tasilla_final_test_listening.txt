TASILLA - A1 FINAL TESTS / LISTENING PATH FINAL TEST
(STEP 27 OF 27 OF THE LISTENING SKILL PATH)
==============================================================
Covers: Topics 1-20. Passing this test completes the
Listening path and feeds the Listening skill score
(gated at 70%).

DESIGN:
- 2 new audios: Audio 1 = slow monologue (Units 1-2
  language), Audio 2 = natural-pace dialogue (Units 3-4

  how the course grew.
- 20 items: 9 on Audio 1 + 9 on Audio 2 + 2 dictations
  (one per audio)
- Pass 70% (14 of 20). Attempts: 2, then teacher unlock.
  Max plays: 2 per audio. XP: 50.

==============================================================

file: audio/a1/finaltest_listening_audio1.mp3, ~45 sec):

  D - R - A. I am 34 years old, and I am from Belem.
  This is my family: my father, Nuno, and my sister,
  Rosa. Rosa is tall, and she has long hair. Our house
  is small - there are two bedrooms. Every day I get
  up in the morning, I drink coffee, and I go to work
  at eight o'clock. Coffee is one dollar at the cafe -
  very cheap!

AUDIO 2 - DIALOGUE (natural pace - Units 3-4 language)
==============================================================

SCRIPT (to record - 2 speakers, natural pace,
file: audio/a1/finaltest_listening_audio2.mp3, ~50 sec):

  Igor: Nena! Are you at the shop?
  Nena: Yes! I am buying a jacket - it is raining, and
        it is very cold this week!
  Igor: What colour?
  Nena: Blue. It is twenty-five dollars - cheap! And
        you? What are you doing?
  Igor: I am at home. I am sick, Nena. I have a
        headache, and I can't go to work.
  Nena: Oh no! Go to bed! Does your brother have your
        medicine?
  Igor: Yes, he does - he is at the pharmacy now. It
        is next to the station.
  Nena: Good. Sleep! And don't watch films all night!
