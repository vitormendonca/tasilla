==============================================================
TASILLA - A1 CONTENT / TOPIC 11: SHOPPING
==============================================================
Unit 3 - Out in the World | Difficulty dial: Topics 11-15
(8 new words, 4 OPTIONS per question, 'not stated' answers
enter, audio ~35s with 2-speaker dialogues, reading texts
50-60 words incl. dialogues, dictation 6 words, free-write)

Grammar embedded: THIS / THAT / THESE / THOSE (G4) +
CAN I...? / CAN YOU...? polite requests (G11) + How much
recycled from T9
Can-do: Buy things, ask prices, sizes, quantities
Recycles: T9 (prices, numbers, pay, cheap/expensive, How
much is/are), T10 (Can I have..., please), T6 (there is/
are), T8 (open days/times)

TARGET LANGUAGE
Words (8): shop, market, open, closed, size, bag, card, cash
Phrases:   Can I help you? / How much is this bag? /
           Can I have this one, please? / Can you buy
           apples for me? / Card or cash? / What size?
Structures: this/that (near/far, singular) +
            these/those (near/far, plural)
            Can I...? / Can you...? + please (requests)
NOTE: clothes and colours NOT here (Topic 18) - shopping
objects are bags, books, food, furniture (all known).
How many + want NOT used (quantity via numbers: three
apples, a big bag of rice).

==============================================================
LESSON 11.1 - VOCABULARY (auto-graded, 8 exercises, all tap)
==============================================================

Intro card:
  shop = you buy things there  |  market = a big open shop,
  many small shops in one place
  open = you can go in :)  |  closed = you cannot go in
  size = small or big  |  bag = you put things in it
  card = plastic money  |  cash = paper money
  + phrase bank: Can I help you? | Card or cash? |
    this bag (here) / that bag (there)
  + grammar note: THIS/THAT + one thing;
    THESE/THOSE + 2, 3, 4... things

EX 1 - Word meaning (MC)
Q: It is Sunday. The shop is not open. It is ___.
   a) closed   [CORRECT]
   b) open
   c) cheap
   d) small
Feedback: Closed = you cannot go in. Open = come in!

EX 2 - Word meaning (MC)
Q: Paper money in your hand is ___.
   a) cash   [CORRECT]
   b) card
   c) size
   d) a menu
Feedback: Cash = paper money. Card = plastic.

EX 3 - Gap-fill (tap the missing word)
Q: My apples, my bread and my rice are in one big ___.
   a) bag   [CORRECT]
   b) shop
   c) card
   d) price
Feedback: A bag = you put your things in it.

EX 4 - Gap-fill (tap the missing word)
Q: There are many small shops with food at the ___.
   a) market   [CORRECT]
   b) size
   c) kitchen
   d) bathroom
Feedback: Market = a big place with many small shops.

EX 5 - Odd one out (MC)
Q: Which word is NOT about shopping?
   a) cash
   b) bedroom   [CORRECT]
   c) price
   d) shop
Feedback: Bedroom is a room at home (Topic 6).

EX 6 - Dialogue completion (MC)
Q: Shop assistant: Good morning! Can I help you?  You: ___
   a) Yes, please. How much is this bag?   [CORRECT]
   b) I get up in the morning.
   c) There are two bathrooms.
   d) My birthday is in May.
Feedback: Can I help you? -> ask about the thing you want.

EX 7 - GRAMMAR: this / these (MC)
Q: ___ bag here is cheap. ___ bags there are expensive!
   a) This / Those   [CORRECT]
   b) These / That
   c) This / That
   d) Those / This
Feedback: THIS + one, near. THOSE + many, there.

EX 8 - GRAMMAR: Can I / Can you (MC)
Q: ___ I have this small bag, please? And ___ you open it?
   a) Can / can   [CORRECT]
   b) Am / are
   c) Is / can
   d) Can / are
Feedback: Requests: Can I have...? Can you open...? + please!

Pass: 70% (6 of 8). XP: 10.

LESSON 11.3 - READING (auto-graded, 2 texts, 3 questions each)
==============================================================

TEXT A (52 words) - Shop notice format:
---------------------------------------
  MODA MIA - YOUR SHOP IN PORTO
  OPEN: Monday to Saturday, nine o'clock to seven o'clock
  CLOSED on Sunday
  * Small bags ...... ten dollars
  * Big bags ........ twenty-five dollars
  * Books and photo frames too!
  Pay by card or cash.
  This Saturday: all small bags five dollars!
---------------------------------------

EX 1 - Gist (MC)
Q: What is this text?
   a) A shop notice with hours and prices   [CORRECT]
   b) A cafe menu
   c) A birthday invitation
   d) A week schedule

EX 2 - Detail (MC)
Q: When is the shop closed?
   a) On Sunday   [CORRECT]
   b) On Saturday
   c) On Monday
   d) The text does not say

EX 3 - Detail: price (MC)
Q: How much are the small bags THIS SATURDAY?
   a) Five dollars   [CORRECT]
   b) Ten dollars
   c) Twenty-five dollars
   d) Fifteen dollars
Feedback: Normal price ten - but THIS Saturday, five!

TEXT B (54 words) - Chat messages format (dialogues enter
reading at this dial):
---------------------------------------
  Zia:   Are you at the market, Bruno?
  Bruno: Yes! It is very big - there are bags, books
         and food!

  Bruno: Yes! Apples are one dollar today - very cheap.
  Zia:   Great! And a big bag of rice, please.
  Bruno: OK! I pay cash - this market is cash only!
---------------------------------------

EX 4 - Gist (MC)

   b) To open a shop
   c) To eat apples
   d) To go home

EX 5 - Detail (MC)
Q: How does Bruno pay at the market?
   a) Cash - the market is cash only   [CORRECT]
   b) By card
   c) He does not pay
   d) The text does not say

EX 6 - NOT STATED (MC)
Q: Is the market open on Sunday?
   a) Yes, it is
   b) No, it is closed
   c) The text does not say   [CORRECT]
   d) Only in the morning
Feedback: The chat says nothing about Sunday - not stated!

LESSON 11.4 - SPEAKING (teacher-graded, 1 recorded task)
==============================================================

PROMPT (no example - free stage):
  Record yourself. You are in a shop. Play BOTH voices
  (assistant and you) - like a mini theater!
  Include:
  1. Assistant: Can I help you?
  2. You: ask the price of one NEAR thing (this/these)
     and one FAR thing (that/those)
  3. Assistant: give two prices
  4. You: choose one, ask for it politely (Can I have...),
     and say card or cash

Max recording: 60 seconds.
Tip: Change your voice a little for each speaker - fun
and clear!

RUBRIC:
  1. this/that/these/those used correctly   [ pass / not yet ]
  2. Polite request with Can I + please     [ pass / not yet ]
  3. Prices with clear numbers (T9)         [ pass / not yet ]
  4. Understandable pronunciation           [ pass / not yet ]

XP: 15 on submit, +10 on approval.
