==============================================================
TASILLA - A1 CONTENT / TOPIC 1: INTRODUCTIONS & GREETINGS
==============================================================
Unit 1 - Foundation | Difficulty dial: Topics 1-5
(6 new words, 3 options per question, slow audio 3-4 sentences,
reading texts 30-40 words, gist + literal questions,
scaffolded writing, dictation 4-5 words)

Grammar embedded: verb TO BE (I am / you are) + subject pronouns (I, you)
Can-do: Greet people, introduce yourself, use basic courtesy phrases

TARGET LANGUAGE
Words (6): hello, hi, goodbye, morning, name, fine
Phrases:   My name is... / Nice to meet you / How are you? /
           I am fine, thank you / Good morning
Structures: I am... / You are... / My name is...

==============================================================
LESSON 1.1 - VOCABULARY (auto-graded, 8 exercises, all tap)
==============================================================

Intro card (shown before exercises):
  hello = a greeting (any time)
  hi = informal hello
  good morning = greeting before 12:00
  goodbye = when you leave
  name = what people call you
  fine = good / OK
  + phrase bank: My name is... | Nice to meet you | How are you?

EX 1 - Word meaning (MC)
Q: You meet a person at 9:00 in the morning. What do you say?
   a) Good morning   [CORRECT]
   b) Goodbye
   c) Fine
Feedback: Good morning = a greeting before 12:00.

EX 2 - Word meaning (MC)
Q: You leave the classroom. What do you say?
   a) Hello
   b) Goodbye   [CORRECT]
   c) My name
Feedback: Goodbye = you say it when you leave.

EX 3 - Gap-fill (tap the missing word)
Q: My ___ is Pedro.
   a) name   [CORRECT]
   b) fine
   c) hello
Feedback: My name is Pedro. = I am Pedro.

EX 4 - Gap-fill (tap the missing word)
Q: A: How are you?  B: I am ___, thank you.
   a) goodbye
   b) fine   [CORRECT]
   c) morning
Feedback: I am fine = I am OK / good.

EX 5 - Odd one out (MC)
Q: Which word is NOT a greeting?
   a) Hello
   b) Hi
   c) Name   [CORRECT]
Feedback: Hello and hi are greetings. Name is not.

EX 6 - Dialogue completion (MC)
Q: A: Hello! My name is Ana.  B: ___
   a) Nice to meet you, Ana!   [CORRECT]
   b) Goodbye, Ana!
   c) Good morning is at 9:00.
Feedback: When a person says their name, answer: Nice to meet you.

EX 7 - GRAMMAR in context: to be (MC)
Q: I ___ Maria.
   a) am   [CORRECT]
   b) is
   c) are
Feedback: With I, always use AM. I am Maria.

EX 8 - GRAMMAR in context: to be (MC)
Q: You ___ my teacher.
   a) am
   b) is
   c) are   [CORRECT]
Feedback: With YOU, always use ARE. You are my teacher.

Pass: 70% (6 of 8). XP: 10.

LESSON 1.4 - SPEAKING (teacher-graded, 1 recorded task)
==============================================================

PROMPT (shown to student):
  Record yourself. Say hello and introduce yourself.
  Say 3 things:
  1. A greeting (Hello / Good morning)
  2. Your name (My name is...)
  3. How you are (I am fine...)
  Example: Hello! My name is Pedro. I am fine, thank you.

Max recording: 30 seconds. Preparation tip shown before recording:
  Practice 2 times before you record. Speak slowly. It is OK!

RUBRIC (visible to student AND teacher):
  1. Says a greeting                       [ pass / not yet ]
  2. Says name with My name is...          [ pass / not yet ]
  3. Uses I am correctly                   [ pass / not yet ]
  4. Understandable pronunciation (A1 bar) [ pass / not yet ]

Teacher action: Approve / Request redo (+ short feedback).
XP: 15 on submit, +10 on approval.

LESSON 1.5 - WRITING (teacher-graded, Mode A typed, SCAFFOLDED)
==============================================================

STEP 1 - MODEL (student reads):
  Hello! Good morning!
  My name is Carla.
  I am fine, thank you.
  Nice to meet you!

STEP 2 - GAP-FILL the model (tap words: name / am / meet / Hello):
  ___! Good morning!            -> Hello
  My ___ is Carla.              -> name
  I ___ fine, thank you.        -> am
  Nice to ___ you!              -> meet
(auto-checked, not scored - warm-up only)

STEP 3 - WRITE YOUR OWN VERSION (typed, teacher-graded):

Minimum requirements:
  - 3 to 4 sentences
  - Use: a greeting, My name is..., I am...

RUBRIC:
  1. Has a greeting                        [ pass / not yet ]
  2. My name is + own name                 [ pass / not yet ]
  3. I am used correctly                   [ pass / not yet ]
