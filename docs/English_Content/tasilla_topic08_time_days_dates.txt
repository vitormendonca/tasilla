==============================================================
TASILLA - A1 CONTENT / TOPIC 8: TIME, DAYS & DATES
==============================================================
Unit 2 - Everyday Life | Difficulty dial: Topics 6-10
(8 new words, 3 options, audio 4-5 sentences ~30s,
reading texts 40-50 words, T/F in reading, dictation 5-6
words, systematic recycling, scaffold fading)

Grammar embedded: TIME PREPOSITIONS (G9): AT + clock time,
ON + day, IN + month/the morning + What time/day questions
with to be
Can-do: Tell the time, say days and months, give simple dates
Recycles: T7 (routine verbs + every day / in the morning /
at night), T2 (numbers as digits, ages), T6 (in + place vs
at/on/in + time), T5 (happy)

TARGET LANGUAGE
Words (8): day, week, month, year, today, time, o'clock,
           birthday
Sets on intro card (learned as lists, like the T2 alphabet):
           Days: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday,
           Friday, Saturday, Sunday
           Months: January - December (exposure + own
           birthday month active)
Phrases:   What time is it? / It is eight o'clock. /
           What day is it today? / Today is Saturday. /
           My birthday is in May. / My class is on Monday
           at seven o'clock.
Structures: at + clock time / on + day / in + month
            What time...? What day...? (to be questions)
NOTE: ordinal dates (May 5th) NOT taught - A1 dates here are
day + month only (in May / on Saturday). Half past / quarter
NOT taught - o'clock and digital style (eight thirty) only.

==============================================================
LESSON 8.1 - VOCABULARY (auto-graded, 8 exercises, all tap)
==============================================================

Intro card:
  day = Monday is a day  |  week = 7 days
  month = May is a month  |  year = 12 months
  today = this day, now  |  time = 8:00 is a time
  o'clock = 8:00 = eight o'clock
  birthday = your special day!
  + sets: the 7 days (audio per day) + the 12 months (list)
  + phrase bank: What time is it? | Today is... |
    at eight o'clock | on Monday | in May

EX 1 - Word meaning (MC)
Q: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday... these are ___ of the week.
   a) days   [CORRECT]
   b) months
   c) years
Feedback: 7 days = 1 week. May, June = months.

EX 2 - Word meaning (MC)
Q: January, May, December... these are ___.
   a) months   [CORRECT]
   b) days
   c) times
Feedback: 12 months = 1 year.

EX 3 - Gap-fill (tap the missing word)
Q: A: What ___ is it?  B: It is eight o'clock.
   a) time   [CORRECT]
   b) day
   c) birthday
Feedback: What TIME is it? -> a clock answer.

EX 4 - Gap-fill (tap the missing word)
Q: ___ is Saturday - no work, no school!
   a) Today   [CORRECT]
   b) Year
   c) O'clock
Feedback: Today = this day, now.

EX 5 - Odd one out (MC)
Q: Which one is a DAY of the week?
   a) May
   b) Friday   [CORRECT]
   c) Year
Feedback: Friday is a day. May is a month.

EX 6 - Dialogue completion (MC)
Q: A: When is your birthday?  B: ___
   a) It is in May.   [CORRECT]
   b) It is eight o'clock.
   c) I get up in the morning.
Feedback: Birthdays -> IN + month.

EX 7 - GRAMMAR: at / on / in (MC)
Q: My class is ___ Monday ___ seven o'clock.
   a) on / at   [CORRECT]
   b) at / on
   c) in / in
Feedback: ON + day. AT + clock time. IN + month.

EX 8 - GRAMMAR: time questions (MC)
Q: A: What ___ is it today?  B: It is Sunday!
   a) day   [CORRECT]
   b) time
   c) month
Feedback: What DAY...? -> Sunday. What TIME...? -> 8 o'clock.

Pass: 70% (6 of 8). XP: 10.

LESSON 8.4 - SPEAKING (teacher-graded, 1 recorded task)

  Record yourself. Talk about your week.
  Say 4 things:
  1. What day is it today? (Today is...)
  2. One thing you do + day + time
     (I study English on Monday at eight o'clock.)
  3. Your happy day of the week + one reason
     (Sunday! I sleep...)

Max recording: 60 seconds.
Tip: Say the day and time TOGETHER: on Monday AT eight.
