1. VOCABULARY REVIEW 6 (auto-graded, 15 exercises)
==============================================================

EX 1 (T16) Q: Saturday + Sunday = the ___.
   a) weekend [CORRECT]  b) week  c) hobby  d) month
EX 2 (T16) Q: ___ you like films? - No, I ___.
   a) Do / don't [CORRECT]  b) Does / doesn't
   c) Are / am not  d) Do / doesn't
EX 3 (T16) Q: My brother ___ play games - he reads.
   a) doesn't [CORRECT]  b) don't  c) isn't  d) not
EX 4 (T17) Q: White, very cold, in winter: ___.
   a) snow [CORRECT]  b) rain  c) wind  d) sun
EX 5 (T17) Q: Look out the window! It ___ right now!
   a) is raining [CORRECT]  b) rains  c) rain
   d) is rain
EX 6 (T17) Q: It ___ every winter in my city.
   a) rains [CORRECT]  b) is raining  c) raining
   d) rain
EX 7 (T18) Q: Snow is ___ and the night is ___.
   a) white / black [CORRECT]  b) black / white
   c) red / blue  d) white / red
EX 8 (T18) Q: Look! She ___ a red dress today!
   a) is wearing [CORRECT]  b) wears  c) wearing
   d) wear
EX 9 (T18) Q: I ___ black shoes to work every day.
   a) wear [CORRECT]  b) am wearing  c) wears
   d) wearing
EX 10 (T19) Q: The medicine shop is the ___.
   a) pharmacy [CORRECT]  b) supermarket  c) bank
   d) station
EX 11 (T19) Q: She ___ a headache, so she ___ come to
   class.
   a) has / can't [CORRECT]  b) have / can
   c) has / doesn't  d) is / can't
EX 12 (T19) Q: One foot, two ___.
   a) feet [CORRECT]  b) foots  c) foot  d) feets
EX 13 (T20) Q: It can fly and it can sing: a ___.
   a) bird [CORRECT]  b) fish  c) cat  d) horse
EX 14 (T20) Q: My ___ name is Bobi. (the dog of my
   family)
   a) dog's [CORRECT]  b) dogs  c) dog is  d) dogs'
EX 15 (T20) Q: Look! The cat ___ with my shoe right now!
   a) is playing [CORRECT]  b) plays  c) play
   d) playing
