==============================================================
TASILLA - A1 CONTENT / TOPIC 2: PERSONAL INFORMATION
==============================================================
Unit 1 - Foundation | Difficulty dial: Topics 1-5
(6 new words, 3 options, slow audio 3-4 sentences,
reading texts 30-40 words, dictation 4-5 words)

Grammar embedded: TO BE questions and negatives (Are you...?
I am not...) + possessive adjectives MY / YOUR
Can-do: Give name, age, nationality, phone, email; spell aloud
Recycles from T1: hello, My name is..., I am...

TARGET LANGUAGE
Words (6): age, country, city, number, email, address
Phrases:   How old are you? / I am ... years old /
           Where are you from? / I am from Brazil /
           What is your phone number? / Can you spell it?
Structures: Are you...? Yes, I am / No, I am not.
            my name, your name, my email, your city
Alphabet:  A-Z for spelling aloud (taught in intro card + audio)

==============================================================
LESSON 2.1 - VOCABULARY (auto-graded, 8 exercises, all tap)
==============================================================

Intro card:
  age = how old you are
  country = Brazil, Japan, Italy...
  city = Sao Paulo, Rome, Tokyo...
  number = 1, 2, 3... your phone has a number
  email = ana@mail.com
  address = where your house is
  + phrase bank: How old are you? | Where are you from? |
    Can you spell it?

EX 1 - Word meaning (MC)
Q: Brazil is a ___.
   a) country   [CORRECT]
   b) city
   c) number
Feedback: Brazil is a country. Sao Paulo is a city.

EX 2 - Word meaning (MC)
Q: ana@mail.com is an ___.
   a) address
   b) email   [CORRECT]
   c) age
Feedback: An email has @ in it.

EX 3 - Gap-fill (tap the missing word)
Q: How old are you? - I am 20 years old. This is my ___.
   a) age   [CORRECT]
   b) email
   c) country
Feedback: Age = how old you are.

EX 4 - Gap-fill (tap the missing word)
Q: What is your phone ___?
   a) city
   b) number   [CORRECT]
   c) country
Feedback: A phone has a number.

EX 5 - Odd one out (MC)
Q: Which one is a CITY?
   a) Japan
   b) Tokyo   [CORRECT]
   c) 25
Feedback: Tokyo is a city. Japan is a country. 25 is a number.

EX 6 - Dialogue completion (MC)
Q: A: Where are you from?  B: ___
   a) I am from Brazil.   [CORRECT]
   b) I am 20 years old.
   c) Nice to meet you.
Feedback: Where are you from? asks about your country or city.

EX 7 - GRAMMAR: to be question (MC)
Q: ___ you from Italy? - No, I am not.
   a) Are   [CORRECT]
   b) Is
   c) Am
Feedback: With YOU, questions use ARE: Are you...?

EX 8 - GRAMMAR: my / your (MC)
Q: A: What is ___ name?  B: ___ name is Pedro.
   a) your / My   [CORRECT]
   b) my / Your
   c) your / Your
Feedback: I ask about YOUR name. You answer with MY name.

Pass: 70% (6 of 8). XP: 10.

==============================================================
LESSON 2.4 - SPEAKING (teacher-graded, 1 recorded task)
==============================================================

PROMPT:
  Record yourself. Give your personal information.
  Say 4 things:
  1. Your name (My name is...)
  2. Your age (I am ... years old)
  3. Your country or city (I am from...)
  4. SPELL your first name (A - N - A ...)
  Example: My name is Ana. I am 25 years old. I am from
  Bertioga. A - N - A.

Max recording: 40 seconds.
Tip: Practice the alphabet letters of YOUR name first.

RUBRIC:
  1. Says name + age with I am           [ pass / not yet ]
  2. Says city/country with I am from    [ pass / not yet ]
  3. Spells own name, letters clear      [ pass / not yet ]
  4. Understandable pronunciation        [ pass / not yet ]

XP: 15 on submit, +10 on approval.
